# Feeding Coordinator and the school calendar

Moved word for word from CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-09. CLAUDE.md keeps the rules most tasks need; this file holds the full rules for the School-Based Feeding Program and the school calendar. Mentions of "the … section" may point to a section now in another file under docs/claude/.

### School Calendar (Calendarific API)

**A holiday, a class suspension and a school break are not feeding days, exactly as a weekend is not.** `App\Support\SchoolCalendar` is the one reading of which weekdays a school is closed, and `FeedingProgramCycle::isFeedingDay($date, $institutionId)` asks it, so `countFeedingDays()`, `endDateIso()`, the Attendance tab's stepping and calendar, the weekday date picker (`data-closed-dates`), the record endpoint's refusal, the Dashboard bar's midnight recount (`data-closed`) and the School Head's "today's session has not been recorded" item all agree. **Always pass the institution** — without it only the national holidays are known. The words for why a date holds no session come from `SchoolCalendar::noSession()` / `reason()`, never typed in a view.

- **National holidays come from the Calendarific API** (`CalendarificClient` → `PublicHolidaySync`, `CALENDARIFIC_API_KEY`, `php artisan holidays:sync`, the System Admin's Sync Holidays button) and carry no `institution_id`. `interpret()` keeps only national, non-working, nationwide days inside the requested year, and **a reply with nothing usable is a failure, never an empty year** — a sync replaces the year's API rows in one transaction, so a bad reply must not wipe them. Calendarific takes the key in the query string, so every failure is logged with it redacted. Only transient statuses are retried; 401/403/429 are answers, and every request spends the free plan's 500 a month (the button is rate-limited to 6 an hour).
- **Suspensions and breaks are the System Admin's** (`SchoolCalendarController`, Control Center → School Calendar), belong to one school, are Auditable with the enterer's name encrypted, and are refused over a day the school recorded attendance on. A synced holiday cannot be removed (the next sync would restore it).
- Until migration `2026_11_01_000001` runs, `SchoolCalendar::supported()` is false and every weekday is a feeding day, as before. `SchoolCalendarTest` guards all of it; `docs/school-calendar-api.md` is the full write-up.

### Feeding Coordinator workflow (attendance-first)

The coordinator's flow is ordered and mostly computed — the period is the `school_year`:

0. **The programme is Junior High only (invariant).** `FeedingBeneficiarySummary::GRADE_LEVELS` (**7–10**) is the single declaration of the grades SBFP covers, and `isEligible($record)` — a covered grade **AND** a qualifying status — is the one test. Senior High is out of scope everywhere it used to leak: no BMI grid, no enrolment candidate, no beneficiary count, and no Grade 11/12 option on the adviser enrolment or account-request forms. Before this, a Grade 11 learner could be enrolled and fed while every report silently dropped them, because `bmiGradeKey()` covered 7–12 and eligibility ignored grade entirely. Never re-type the range — read the constant.
0a. **Qualifying is not enrolment (invariant).** The class adviser measures a learner and their nutritional status decides whether they **qualify** (wasted / severely wasted / underweight); the Feeding Coordinator then decides whether to **enrol** them. Only an enrolled learner is a beneficiary — `student_health_records.feeding_enrolled_at` (plain, indexed) with `feeding_enrolled_by` (encrypted staff name). **A measurement never enrols anybody, and enrolment needs no parent's consent.** **Nor does a measurement end anybody's place:** `storeEndline` overwrites `nutritional_status`, so `isBeneficiary()` asks whether a learner qualified at either weighing, and one re-measured as Normal stays a beneficiary on every screen until the coordinator removes them. `isEligible()` keeps reading the current status, because it answers who may be given a place now. Never build the roll as `isEligible` AND `isEnrolled`, or as qualified minus waiting: six screens did, and each dropped the child who recovered and took them out of the outcome figures. `FeedingRecoveredBeneficiaryTest` guards it through the real endline form. The SBFP consent gate (`FeedingConsent`, the adviser's "School-Based Feeding Program consent" upload) was removed on 2026-11-03: enrolling is the coordinator's decision alone, every qualified learner on the waiting list carries Enroll, and the Nutritional Health Status list reads "Qualified, awaiting enrolment". Rows filed as `ParentalConsentForm::PROGRAM_FEEDING` while the gate existed are kept and read by nothing. Do not re-add a consent check to enrolment without asking; `FeedingEnrollmentTest::no_parents_consent_is_needed_to_enrol` guards it. The adviser's student profile carries no separate Parent's Consent button either: the consent is read and uploaded on its Consent tab. **The class adviser's Nutritional Health Status page** (`feedingStatus`) reads the same: *Enrolled* is `FeedingBeneficiarySummary::isEnrolled()`, never "measured as wasted", and *Completed* is `ProfileCompletionRule::isComplete()` — the cycle has run its feeding days, the endline is recorded and the learner attended — never "an endline weight is on file". (The nurse's Fill Medical Record used to stamp the endline columns on every examination, copying the baseline status across as if the learner had been re-measured; it no longer does unless an older form posts a height or weight, `NurseExaminationIntegrityTest`, but rows written before that fix may still carry an endline status with no endline weight); a row short of either says why underneath (`AdviserFeedingStatusTest`). `FeedingProgramController::isEnrolledBeneficiary()` is the one test: the dashboard's panels, the Feeding Program roster and the record-attendance screen all read the enrolled roll, while **attendance import and photo scanning deliberately still match the whole roster** (a sheet is evidence of who was fed; dropping a name for a missing enrolment would lose it). Enrolment happens in the dashboard's modal via `FeedingEnrollmentController` (`candidates` / `store`) — additive, idempotent (re-enrolling is a no-op, never a second stamp), scoped, transactional and audited. **Its scope is required, not optional:** `schoolRecords()` returns nothing without an `active_institution_id` rather than falling through to every school's children, which is what makes the adviser-to-coordinator handover exact — an adviser's entry reaches the waiting list because the adviser stamped their own `institution_id` on it, so it appears for that school's coordinator and no other. The dialog reloads on open and rides the host page's `fc:records-changed` pulse, so a learner measured mid-session appears without a reload. The migration backfilled every already-qualified learner as enrolled at their record's creation, because until then qualifying *was* enrolment. `FeedingEnrollmentTest` guards this.
0b. **The cycle length is the Division's, not the application's (invariant).** Division policy says 120 feeding days and 90 has been under discussion, so a figure compiled in is a figure a school cannot correct when its Division settles the question. `institutions.feeding_cycle_days` overrides `config('feeding.cycle_days')`; read it through **`FeedingProgramCycle::durationDays()`** on the instance, or `durationForInstitution($id)` where there is no instance — `FeedingProgramCycle::DURATION_DAYS` is now only the app default and must never be printed as the school's own figure. Every screen that says "day N of M" reads it that way, the adviser's endline gate included. `FeedingExcusedAbsenceTest` guards it.
0c. **A weekend is not a feeding day (invariant).** Nobody is fed on a Saturday or a Sunday, so a weekend is not a session the school missed — it is not a session at all, and counting it stretched every "day N of 120" past where the programme actually was. `FeedingProgramCycle::isFeedingDay()` is the one test and `countFeedingDays()` the one count; `day()` counts the learner's **school days** from the first recorded session, `endDateIso()` is the date of the 120th of them (about 24 calendar weeks out, never `start + 119 days`), and `storeRecordedAttendance` refuses a weekend date outright. The Attendance tab steps over a weekend rather than onto it, says so on the sheet, and offers no way to record one. **Nor can a weekend be picked:** the feeding-date inputs (the sheet's `#faDate`, the record screen's `#sessionDate`) carry `data-weekday-picker` and are upgraded by `partials/weekday-date-picker` — a month grid where Saturdays, Sundays and days outside the input's min/max are disabled buttons, the arrow keys step over them, and a pick sets the hidden native input and dispatches `change` so the page's own listeners run unchanged (without JS the native input is left as it was). The Calendar view draws a weekend as a locked cell, never a link; a legacy weekend mark still shows its glyph there. A legacy weekend mark (written before the guard) is read as the Monday after, so a started programme never reports day 0. `FeedingCoordinatorTabsTest` guards all of it.
1. **Attendance first.** Three paths, all landing in `feeding_attendances` inside one transaction (no partial writes), each recording an `AttendanceImport` batch. `AttendanceImport::existsForPeriod()` is the gate.
   - **Spreadsheet:** `importAttendance` parses a DMIRIE-style CSV/XLSX (`App\Support\AttendanceSheetParser` — NAME/GRADE/SECTION + dated columns) and matches rows to learners.
   - **Photograph:** `scanAttendancePhoto` sends the photo to Claude vision **together with the roster** (`App\Support\AttendanceSheetScanner`) so the model matches a mark to an already-known learner instead of reading names cold — deliberately more reliable than blind OCR, and it can't invent a learner. It is never used to identify anyone from a face. Requires `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`; absent one, the route is disabled and the spreadsheet path is unaffected.
   - **Recorded on site:** `recordAttendanceForm` / `storeRecordedAttendance` (`feedingcor-dashboard/record-attendance.blade.php`) — one screen listing every beneficiary, one tap per learner, one save, reached from the dashboard's "Record Today's Attendance". **It sits under the Attendance tab** — the rail is passed `'active' => 'attendance'` and the breadcrumb leads back there. It used to highlight Feeding Program, the one tab that reads the marks and deliberately offers no way to change one, so the rail named the tab that cannot do what the screen is for; the `#record-attendance` hand-off that opened Feeding Program's upload modal is gone with it. Marks are confirmed on arrival (`SOURCE_MANUAL_ENTRY`, `needs_review` false) and supersede an unread scanned mark for the same session. **The marks are read back before they are written** (`partials/attendance-confirm-modal` + `css/feeding-attendance-confirm.css`): a recorded session is closed as a whole, and a wrong mark is only put right afterwards on the learner's own beneficiary record, so the save is confirmed in a dialog naming **every learner and the mark entered for them**, with the tally above it. It is one partial wired to `#recordForm` by id, inlined by both surfaces that carry the form — the Attendance tab's dialog and this standalone screen — so the two confirm the same way; it reads the form rather than the server, writes nothing itself and adds no second path into `feeding_attendances`. It stacks above the record dialog (Back must return the coordinator to the marks) and is not drawn at all once a session is closed. **A learner left unmarked writes no row at all** — never an absence; the coordinator may simply have skipped them, and they are counted on the confirmation rather than listed on it. An absence may carry a `remarks` reason and every mark carries `recorded_by_name` (who took it): both are personal information, cast `EncryptedString`, and because all three bulk writes are **upserts (which bypass model casts)** they are encrypted in the controller with `Crypt::encryptString` — do the same for any column added to those writes. A learner marked present stores no remark. `FeedingRecordAttendanceTest` guards this.
1a. **An excused absence is not held against anybody (invariant).** A learner may miss a session for a reason the school accepts — fasting during Ramadan, illness, a family emergency — and the coordinator calls that list the **buffer**. So a confirmed mark has **three** states, not two: present / absent (unexcused) / **excused**, carried as `feeding_attendances.is_excused` beside `is_present` and never folded into it (the learner still was not fed, and the sheet has to keep saying so). **`App\Support\FeedingAttendanceMark` is the one reading of a mark** — `state()` returns present / absent / excused / unconfirmed / not_marked, and **`forRule()` is the projection every calculation must go through**, collapsing excused *and* unconfirmed to NULL so both are excluded from the numerator and the denominator alike. Never write `needs_review ? null : is_present` again; that line existed in fourteen places, and a third state added to fourteen copies is a third state that disagrees with itself. Any lean `get([...])` feeding a mark reading must select `is_excused` when the column exists. Recording an excused absence happens in the same one dialog and the same one endpoint every other mark does, and correcting a mark to Excused happens on the learner's beneficiary record where it is attributed and audited — there is deliberately **no bulk "excuse everyone"**, because an excuse is a reason given for one named learner. `FeedingExcusedAbsenceTest` guards it.
2. **At-risk is derived, never tagged — and nobody is flagged without the coordinator's confirmation (invariant).** Only attendance decides; nutritional status never auto-flags a learner. The flow is the one the **capstone adviser** set, and **`App\Support\FeedingAttendanceFlag` is the one reading of it** — every screen that counts, lists, badges or stores a flag reads it, never `FeedingAtRiskRule` alone:
   - **Day 3** of unexcused absence in a row (`absence_notice_days`, default **3**) → **Attendance Notice**: the coordinator is asked to verify the record. **Nobody is flagged.**
   - **Day 7** (`absence_flag_days`, default **7**, under the `unexcused_absence_days` mode) → **Awaiting Confirmation**: the threshold is reached (`FeedingAtRiskRule::thresholdReached()` — renamed from `isAtRisk()` precisely so no caller can mistake it for a flag). **Still nobody is flagged.**
   - The coordinator **confirms the record is correct** → **Flagged for Follow-Up**. Only a `feeding_attendance_confirmations` row at the `flag` stage flags anybody (`FeedingAttendanceConfirmation`, Auditable, staff name and remark encrypted). A `notice`-stage row is the verification at day 3: it acknowledges that run's notice and flags nobody; it is keyed to the run by `run_started_on`, so it never covers the next run, and it never stands in for the day-7 confirmation.
   - **A flag stays until a Resolved follow-up closes it** — whether or not the learner comes back the next day, because putting a child on the follow-up list and taking them off it are both human decisions. Once closed, the marks it covered sit behind a cutoff (`max(confirmed_through, resolution date)`), so the same week of absence cannot flag the learner twice; a fresh run starts the flow from day 1.
   - **Enrolment is never touched** by a notice, a threshold, a confirmation or a follow-up — see 2b.
   - **Only the Feeding Coordinator and the School Nurse see who is flagged** (`FeedingAttendanceFlag::VISIBLE_TO`). The Clinic Teacher (nurse's dashboard KPI, Nutritional Status Report, Nutritional Health Status list), Clinic Staff (their dashboard's alerts) and the class adviser (roster meta, Feeding Status page, student profile) are never handed a learner's flag — the server does not compute it for them rather than computing and hiding it. The School Head sees **counts only** (dashboard KPI and attention queue, programme figures) and never a list of flagged names; the Nutritional Health Status list's attendance standing, filter and export column exist only for the nurse.
   - The stored `student_health_records.is_at_risk` holds **FLAGGED only**, rewritten by `FeedingAttendanceFlag::refreshStoredFlags()` after every attendance write, every confirmation and every Resolved follow-up; the clinic dashboards count it in SQL.
   **Which test reaches the threshold is itself school-configurable** (`institutions.feeding_at_risk_mode`). Three modes — `attendance_rate` (the app default, **< 80%**), `unexcused_absence_days` (the adviser's run of `absence_flag_days`) and `consecutive_absences`; the notice and the confirmation apply in **every** mode. All the figures, `feeding_absence_notice_days` included, are set through the same audited System Admin form (`dashboard.system-admin.institutions.at-risk-threshold`), which refuses a notice at or past the flag; NULL means the app default. **Sta. Ana's own row carries `unexcused_absence_days`** — set by `Institution::seedDefaults()` and by `2026_09_08_000001_set_deployment_school_at_risk_rule`. The app default stays the rate for anybody else. The **removal review** (`absence_removal_days`, `needsRemovalReview()`) was **retired**: an attendance count no longer suggests taking anybody off the feeding line; the column stays on `institutions` and nothing reads it. Always read the rule through **`FeedingAtRiskRule::forInstitution($institutionId)`**. `describeThreshold()` / `thresholdLabel()` / `noticeLabel()` name the rule in its own unit ("7 days of consecutive unexcused absence", "80%"), so a card and a sentence cannot name different rules. `FeedingAttendanceFlagTest` and `FeedingAtRiskTabTest` guard the flow, `FeedingFlagVisibilityTest` who sees it, `FeedingAtRiskRuleTest` the rule, `FeedingAtRiskThresholdTest` the per-school override, `FeedingExcusedAbsenceTest` the time-based mode.
2a. **A rate is not a classification until there is history behind it (invariant).** The rate test applies only after a **minimum observation period** — `minimum_observation_days` (default **10**), school-configurable (`institutions.feeding_min_observation_days`; NULL = app default, 0 or 1 = classify from the first confirmed session). One of four recorded sessions is 25% and fails every threshold, but four sessions is a sample, not a finding. Inside the window a learner reads **Early Monitoring** and `thresholdReached()` returns false. The window is counted in the learner's own **confirmed** sessions. **A run of absences carries its own evidence**, so the run-based modes need only one confirmed session (`usesObservationWindow()` is true for the rate alone) — holding a seven-day run back for a ten-day window would tell the coordinator a week late. The rate is still computed and shown throughout. `FeedingAtRiskRuleTest` guards this.
2b. **Leaving the programme is a human decision with a reason (invariant).** A beneficiary who stops coming is taken off the active list once the class adviser has confirmed they are not returning, and the vacated slot goes to somebody on the waiting list. Two things hold: **attendance never removes anybody** — being below the threshold is a reason to follow a learner up, never a reason to stop feeding them, so no rule calls `FeedingEnrollmentController::remove()` and the At-Risk tab still renders no enrolment endpoint at all; and **removal is a stamp beside the enrolment, never a deletion of it** — `student_health_records.feeding_removed_at` (plain, indexed) with `feeding_removed_by` / `feeding_removal_reason` (encrypted), because the learner *was* fed and the record has to go on saying so. **`FeedingBeneficiarySummary::isEnrolled()` is the one test for "on the feeding line today"** (enrolled AND not removed) and `isBeneficiary()` reads it; never test `feeding_enrolled_at !== null` directly again. Removal is offered on one learner's beneficiary record and nowhere else, requires a reason, is scoped, transactional and audited. A removed learner returns to the waiting list — which is exactly where the substitute comes from — and re-enrolling through the one enrolment endpoint clears the removal rather than leaving a learner both enrolled and removed.
2c. **A learner nobody recorded is marked Not Recorded (invariant).** `FeedingAttendanceMark::NOT_RECORDED` is the one word for it, on every screen and every role's, and it is a missing record, never an absence: no row is written and no rate or flag moves. `FeedingProgramCycle::dueDates()` is the one list of days a learner should have a mark for: every session anybody recorded, plus every feeding day since the first of them that ended with nothing recorded (a day's attendance cannot be entered afterwards, so it stays that way). Today joins only once somebody has marked it, a weekend or closed day never does, and a school with no session has none. The per-learner count, the By Beneficiary history, Attendance History, the Calendar, the beneficiary record and the School Head's figure all read it, so they agree; **days completed** on the beneficiary record and the head's feeding-day count stay distinct recorded sessions, and the exported DepEd form keeps a column per recorded session and a blank cell for no mark. A past school year lists only the sessions recorded that year. `FeedingNotRecordedTest` guards it.
3. **An unconfirmed mark is not an absence (invariant).** `feeding_attendances.is_present` is **nullable**: NULL means a scanned mark no human has confirmed. `FeedingAtRiskRule` excludes NULLs from both the numerator and the denominator, so an unreadable photo can neither flag nor unflag a learner. Never aggregate attendance with `SUM(CASE WHEN is_present ...)` — that folds NULL into "absent". Unclear marks surface in the review queue (`attendanceReviewQueue` → `resolveAttendanceReview`), every resolution is attributed and audit-logged, and the scanned photo is deleted once all its marks are confirmed. `FeedingAttendanceScanTest` guards this.
4. **The dashboard is live, filtered and derived** (`FeedingCoordinatorController::dashboard` → `feed-dashboard.blade.php`). The SBFP header counts the feeding day from `App\Support\FeedingProgramCycle` (day 1 = first recorded session, shared with the Feeding Program page), with the percentage beside the bar. Below it sit four cards (Beneficiary / Attendance / At-Risk / Awaiting Enrollment), an **Attendance Monitoring** panel for today's session and a **Nutritional Status** roll. Its **Record Today's Attendance** opens the shared Record Attendance dialog **here**, for today — it used to send the coordinator to the Attendance tab to press a second button, a tab change and a page load to reach the control this one already names. `buildRecordSession()` supplies it, on the first paint only and never on the 20-second pulse (it holds marks nobody has saved yet), reading the **whole enrolled roll unfiltered** and the same three gates the tab applies: a day that has ended on the school's clock is closed (`FeedingAttendanceWindow`), a weekend is not a session, and a date outside the running cycle is not either — and **the button is drawn every day, recorded or not**: on a closed day the same dialog opens on the session as a *record* — the marks it came to, the reasons filed with them, and a line saying why nothing more can be entered — because a control that vanishes leaves a coordinator with nothing to press and no way to tell "already done" from "broken". In that state the partial renders no form element at all (`$recordCanSave` false): no radios, no remark fields, no bulk marks, no Save and no read-back dialog, and `storeRecordedAttendance` refuses the write regardless. An unconfirmed scanned mark leaves the session open. The save posts `return_to=dashboard` and lands back here.
   - **Five coordinated filters** (`readFilters`): School Year, Grade, Section, **Sex**, Attendance Status. Only the school year is a SQL filter (`school_year` is plain); the rest are applied in PHP because their columns are encrypted. Year/grade/section/sex scope **every** panel; the attendance status narrows the attendance roll alone, so its headline keeps counting everyone expected today. There is deliberately **no Nutritional Status filter** here: the panel below the roll already breaks the school down by status, over a population the toolbar does not decide, and a sixth control answering the same question about a different roll is how one screen ends up disagreeing with itself. A `status` off the query string decides nothing — `readFilters` no longer reads the key at all. The panel's subtitle reads **"Learners by baseline status"**, not "Beneficiaries", because its Population control can widen it to the whole school. **Sex is read through `FeedingBeneficiarySummary::sexOf()` everywhere** — it lives in the encrypted `student_details.gender` blob and advisers have typed "M", "male" and "Male", so one normalizer is what stops four tabs disagreeing about who is in a filtered roll. Every coordinator surface carries the filter: Dashboard, Attendance, At-Risk, Beneficiaries, the Feeding Program roster and the enrolment dialog. **The filter is labelled "Gender" on screen** — every one of them, plus the School Head's Masterlist and the nurse's Student Health Records — while the query key stays `sex` and the data stays `student_details.gender`; the label is the only thing that changed, so never rename the key to match it. The one place still labelled "Sex" is the DepEd form facsimiles (the BMI grid, Form 1's Master List of Beneficiaries, the head's exported grid), which reproduce printed government paper and must read as it does. Section options are rebuilt server-side for the chosen grade.
   - **Nutritional Status** counts a chosen population by status over `nutritionScale()` — Severely Wasted / Wasted / Normal / Obese / Not measured. Underweight is folded into Wasted (`panelStatus()`) exactly as the DepEd BMI reports do, so the breakdown always sums to the total; Zero rows still render. Two controls sit on it. **Population** (`?population=beneficiaries|all_students`) switches between the programme (qualified **and** enrolled) and **All Students** — every learner in a covered grade, which is the only place a Normal or Obese learner is counted, since they are never beneficiaries and are still the school's children. It moves this panel **alone**: the cards above it are the programme's figures and must not change because somebody widened one breakdown. It is a link group, not a `<select>`, because the panel is replaced wholesale by the live refresh and a select's selection would not survive that. **Endline** is a second count column beside Baseline, never merged with it: it counts only learners actually re-measured, so it may sum to less than the total and reports how many are still to come. Under All Students a learner nobody weighed is **Not measured**, never Normal — `panelStatusOrUnmeasured()` exists for exactly that, because "412 Normal" that quietly includes 60 unweighed children is the one reading this panel must not produce (for beneficiaries the row is always 0: one qualified on a measurement).
   - **Attendance Monitoring** shows Student / Grade / Section / Attendance / Remarks. A recorded session leaves each learner **Present or Absent**; an unread scanned mark reads `Unconfirmed` and a learner no sheet has covered reads `Not Recorded`, never an absence. The headline is always `present / expected — %` (greyed until the session is recorded), with chips for the other states.
   - **Attendance At Risk** (`buildAttendanceRisk`) is the coordinator's work list from `FeedingAttendanceFlag` — learners **flagged**, **awaiting confirmation** or under an **attendance notice**, the decision waiting on them first — as Student / Grade / Section / In a Row / Attendance / Status / Action, under a headline printing the school's `Notice at` and `Flag at` figures and the three counts. Days present is counted over **confirmed** sessions. The card's At-Risk figure counts **flagged** learners only, computed live. "View" opens `feedingcor-program.attendance.learner` — one learner's sessions, keyed by **record id, never by name** and re-scoped to the coordinator's school. "View At-Risk List" goes to the At-Risk Students tab.
   - **Nutritional Progress** (`App\Support\FeedingNutritionProgress`) plots baseline against endline per status and computes `Improved Nutritional Status: n / total — %`. The rule is **in the business logic, never hand-entered**: improvement means climbing the wasting scale (Severely Wasted → Wasted → Normal); overshooting into Obese/Overweight is deliberately *not* improvement, and the denominator is every beneficiary (not only those measured) so the headline cannot creep up simply because few endline readings exist. Two series on the theme's validated `--series-risk` / `--series-healthy` pair — colour carries baseline-vs-endline, the row label carries the status, every bar is direct-labelled, and a `<details>` table view repeats every number. Re-run the dataviz validator before changing either colour.
   - **Enroll beneficiary** sits on the progress row and opens the enrolment modal (`#enrollBackdrop`) — eyebrow / title / weigh-in line, four client-side filters (name, grade, section, status; names are encrypted so the server cannot search them), per-row **Enroll** and bulk **Enroll selected**, and a footer reading "N currently enrolled · M waiting". It reuses the Feeding Program modal anatomy (`.modal-backdrop` / `.modal-panel` / `-head` / `-body` / `-foot`) so a dialog looks the same product-wide. While open it rides the dashboard pulse (`fc:records-changed`) instead of polling on its own timer, so a learner an adviser measures mid-session appears without a reload; enrolling dispatches `fc:refresh-request` so the cards behind it re-read at once.
   - All five panels are `live-pane`s: the page polls `dashboard.feedingcor.metrics.pulse` (a stamp, no PII — exempt in `AuditSensitiveAccess`) every 20s and re-renders from `dashboard.feedingcor.metrics`, **forwarding its own query string** so a refresh redraws the filtered view. That endpoint returns the **same Blade partials** the first paint used (`feedingcor-dashboard/partials/*`), so live and reloaded views cannot drift. `FeedingCoordinatorDashboardTest` guards this.
5. **The Beneficiaries tab opens on the programme's five figures** (`StudentHealthRecordController::feedingHealthRecords` → `feedingcor-dashboard/feed-healthrec.blade.php`). Under the SBFP header (`SBFP <span>Beneficiaries</span>`, school year, `Active Program:`) sit five cards — Total Beneficiaries / Severely Wasted / Wasted / At Risk / Currently Attending — rendered from `partials/beneficiary-cards` and computed by **`App\Support\FeedingBeneficiarySummary`**, the one place that decides who is a beneficiary (qualified **and** enrolled), which status they are reported under (Underweight folds into Wasted) and how attendance is counted (NULL is unconfirmed and votes neither way). The Dashboard reads the same class, so the two tabs cannot report different programme sizes — `FeedingBeneficiaryCardsTest` guards that equality. **The At Risk card's hint names the school's own rule** (`at_risk_threshold_label`, from `FeedingAtRiskRule::describeThreshold()`): it printed "Below 80% attendance" whatever the school was set to, so at a school flagged on a week of unexcused absence the label named a threshold the figure above it was not judged against. Never type a threshold into a card. The cards follow the tab's grade/section filter and are re-read from `dashboard.feedingcor-health-records.cards` whenever the coordinator's metrics pulse moves; that endpoint returns the same Blade partial the first paint used. Nothing here is stored or hand-entered — never add a card backed by a persisted counter. Under the cards sits a **three-view tab rail** (`partials/beneficiary-tabs`, `?view=all|all_students|at_risk`): **All beneficiaries** (the enrolled roll — always the Total Beneficiaries card), **All Students** (every learner on the roll) and **At risk** (always the At Risk card). `?view=pending` was this middle view's old name, when it listed only the qualified learners waiting to be enrolled; old links still resolve to `all_students`. Each tab carries its count, the counts respect the grade/section filter exactly as the cards do, and the tab filters the table below it — so a tab's number is always the number of rows that view lists (`FeedingBeneficiaryCardsTest` asserts count == rows for all three). Every figure is derived at read time from the same sources the tab reads, so a learner's page and their row can never disagree; the rate is `FeedingAtRiskRule::forInstitution()`'s, an unconfirmed mark votes neither way and is never rendered as an absence, and no rate at all is an em dash. 7. **Attendance is its own tab** (`FeedingAttendanceController` → `feedingcor-dashboard/attendance.blade.php`, `css/feeding-attendance.css`, sidebar key `attendance`). It answers **who → when → present/absent → cumulative rate → at risk**, and deliberately nothing else: no baseline, BMI, height-for-age, endline, consent or medical column appears here, because a learner's health profile is the Beneficiaries tab's responsibility and two tabs owning one fact is how they start disagreeing (`FeedingAttendanceTabTest` asserts those words never render).    **A session is open on its own day and closed after it, recorded or not** (`App\Support\FeedingAttendanceWindow` — the one reading of "is it still today", **on the school's clock**, read by the endpoint, the correction endpoint, this tab, the Dashboard, the standalone screen and the At-Risk verification dialog; the server's UTC date is never asked, since before 8 AM in Davao it is still yesterday): learners arrive late while the session is still being served, so on the day the same dialog reopens as *Edit Attendance* on the marks on file (`recordEditing`; no Clear — a blank row writes nothing, so a cleared mark would stay on file), and `storeRecordedAttendance` writes **only the marks that changed** (`changedMarks()`), so every other mark keeps who took it, an edit adds no second `AttendanceImport` batch, and the audit entry carries each changed mark's old and new value (never the reason). `sessionConfirmed` says a human has confirmed a mark for the date; `sessionClosed` says its day has ended. Four views on the shared `.seg-tabs` rail: **Attendance Sheet** (the session as a record, searchable, with a tally of what it came to), **Attendance History** (one row per feeding day, newest first, each linking to its sheet), **By Beneficiary** (the cumulative standing the school's threshold is applied to) and **Calendar** (the month, with complete / incomplete / no-session days). **A day on the Calendar opens what happened on it** (`partials/attendance-day-modal`): every weekday that has happened and that the school did not close is a button, and the dialog lists who was present, who was absent or excused with the reason recorded, whose scanned mark is unread, and who nobody marked — a learner on the roll that a day does not name is *Not Recorded*, never absent, and that holds on a day nobody recorded at all (the dialog lists the whole roll, and the cell is tagged). Three things are deliberately read off the **unfiltered** roll on every view: `filterOptions` (options built from a filtered list delete the very choice that would widen it again), `sessionLocked` (a session's standing is a fact about the day, and narrowing to one section must never reopen it) and `recordRows` (the dialog lists everyone). **The mark and the standing are two questions, so they are two controls.** **Attendance** offers exactly **All / Present / Absent** (`MARK_FILTERS`), because those are the only three answers a confirmed mark has; "Not yet recorded" and "Unconfirmed" are states a row can be in and are printed on the row, but neither is an answer to "who came today", and a value off the wire that is not on the list is dropped. **Status** — the `standing` query key, `STANDING_FILTERS`: at risk (flagged) / awaiting confirmation / attendance notice / early monitoring — is a verdict across the programme rather than a mark on one day, so it is its own control, rendered **only on By Beneficiary** — the roll it applies to — and labelled after the Status column it narrows; the key stays `standing`, so never rename it to match the label.
