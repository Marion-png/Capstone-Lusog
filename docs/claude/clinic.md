# Clinic

Moved word for word from CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-09. CLAUDE.md keeps the rules most tasks need; this file holds the full rules for the clinic: Clinic Teacher, incident reports, data visualization, the RxNorm allergy check, consultations, vital signs, Sheet 2, the MLAT and medicine stock. Mentions of "the … section" may point to a section now in another file under docs/claude/.

### Clinic Teacher (the nurse's screens, one medicine)

A teacher who covers the school clinic when the nurse is not in it. The role is **not a narrower Clinic Staff** — it reads the School Nurse's whole module set (the clinic dashboard, health records, the assessment queue, Fill/Edit Medical Record, the consultation log, the nutrition module, consent forms, the inventory, data visualization) — and it is not a nurse either. Two boundaries make it its own role, and both are enforced on the write:

- **It dispenses paracetamol and nothing else.** `App\Support\DispensingRights` is the one reading of who may hand a medicine over and which: the nurse has the whole shelf; a Clinic Teacher and Clinic Staff have paracetamol and nothing else (`RESTRICTED_ROLES`). Clinic Staff were admitted on these same terms on 2026-10-26 — before that they dispensed nothing. Paracetamol is matched on the **name**, never on an id or an exact catalogue string — the DepEd list carries it twice already and a school may hold a third spelling, so an allow-list of ids would silently narrow to whichever rows existed when it was written. The New Consultation dialog lists the **whole** shelf, paracetamol first for every role (`DispensingRights::ordered()`, through the one shared `partials/consultation-medicine-options`), so the teacher can see what the clinic holds, disables what this role may not give and prints the restriction under the field; none of that is the enforcement. `ConsultationController` asks `DispensingRights::allows()` again **inside the transaction**, against the row it actually read, so a replayed form or a crafted `medicine_id` is refused — and because it throws there, a refused dispense takes the consultation down with it and leaves no visit and no stock movement to reconcile. `ClinicTeacherRoleTest` pins the crafted-request case and the rollback.
- **It reads the shelf; it does not change what the school owns.** `MedicineInventoryController` now splits one guard into two — `VIEW_ROLES` (the whole clinic, including this role) and `STOCK_WRITE_ROLES` (not this role) — so Add Medicine and Receive Stock are refused at the endpoint as well as being absent from the page. Stock still moves for this role through the one path it is allowed: a dispense on a consultation.

Everything else is deliberately **shared, not copied**. `App\Support\ClinicDashboard::read()` is the one reading behind both the nurse's dashboard and this role's, so two desks cannot report different figures for one clinic on the same afternoon, and the nutrition module, the masterlist, the feeding-programme page and the nurse's Forms & Templates (`FeedingCoordinatorController::sbfpForms`, `dashboard.clinic-teacher.forms`) are the same controllers rendered in this role's rail (`partials/clinic-teacher-sidebar`, dispatched by `partials/clinic-rail`). The role's URLs live under `/dashboard/clinic-teacher` because `EnsureActiveSession` seeds a prototype session for whichever role a URL belongs to — a demo Clinic Teacher opening `/dashboard/school-nurse` would be re-seeded as the nurse before the page rendered. The feeding-programme page is read-only for this role exactly as it is for the nurse. **It charts incident reports** on the learner profile's Incident Reports tab — an incident does not wait for the nurse to come back — and reads every report there, the nurse's included; its reports are signed *Clinic Teacher*. **Withdrawing one stays the nurse's** (`StudentIncidentReport::WITHDRAW_ROLES`, narrower than `WRITE_ROLES`): the endpoint refuses the teacher, and the panel reads `can_withdraw` from the server rather than drawing Withdraw for whoever may file. **Clinic Staff chart one too** (`WRITE_ROLES`, since 2026-11-03), signed *Clinic Staff*, and read every report; withdrawing stays the nurse's for them as for the teacher. **Editing one is the nurse's and the clinic teacher's** (`EDIT_ROLES`, `student-incidents.update`, the Edit button on each report, read from `can_edit`): it reopens the one filing form on that report, on any report on the learner's profile, under the rules filing has. The signature stands, because who filed it is never read off the request, so the edit stamp (`edited_by_name` encrypted, `edited_by_role`, `edited_at`; migration `2026_11_04_000001`, guarded by `supportsEditStamp()`) prints who changed it beside the signature. A value is assigned only when it differs, so the audit entry names only what changed and a save that changed nothing stamps nothing. Clinic Staff file but do not edit. `StudentIncidentReportTest` guards it.

### Incident Reports (physical examination, printable form, AI check)

The profile's Incident Reports tab (`StudentIncidentReportController`, `partials/student-incidents-panel` + `-script`, `css/student-incidents.css`) charts in F-DAR. Three things were added on a tester's request (2026-11-08); each has one owner:

- **The physical examination is the objective half of Data**, charted field by field under the D row: temperature, pulse, respiratory rate, blood pressure (`App\Rules\BloodPressure`), SpO₂, level of consciousness (AVPU), pain score 0–10, body part affected (suggested from a datalist, never enforced) and findings. **`App\Support\IncidentPhysicalExam` is the one declaration** — rules, ranges, labels, the stored shape (`fromInput()`), the read-back (`summary()`) — read by the form, the endpoint, the list and the printed form. Every reading is optional; one nobody took is blank, never refused or invented. Stored as one encrypted JSON column, `student_incident_reports.physical_exam` (`EncryptedArray`; migration `2026_11_08_000001`, guarded by `StudentIncidentReport::supportsPhysicalExam()` — until it runs the form draws no section and a report still files). An edit compares the section as a whole, so saving it unchanged stamps nothing.
- **The report prints on the school's Incident Report form** (`student-incidents.print`, `dashboard/incident-report-print.blade.php`): the office letterhead (`SchoolLetterhead::masthead()`), learner, incident (tick boxes for nature, severity, guardian informed), physical examination, the F-DAR chart with who charted it, and **four signatories on one row — Clinic Nurse, Class Adviser, Curriculum Head, School Principal** (`StudentIncidentReport::SIGNATORIES`). Names come from accounts: the charting nurse (or the school's nurse when a clinic teacher or clinic staff charted it, whose name stays on the chart), the learner's adviser (`SchoolSignatories::classAdviser()`, matched without regard to case or spacing) and the school head. No account is a curriculum head, so that line is left to sign on — never an invented name. Every reader may print (the adviser signs it); **Print Blank Form** (`student-incidents.blank`) is the clinic's — the same form with the learner's lines filled and every incident line ruled, for an incident written up by hand. Both print through a hidden frame (the profile stays open), are scoped like every read, audited as `printed`, and fit one A4 page — check with headless Chrome's `--print-to-pdf` after changing the layout, **with** the header and footer left on (no `--no-pdf-header-footer`), because that is what a school printer gets. **The page has no margin (`@page { margin: 0 }`)**: a browser prints its own date, page title, web address and page number in the page margin, and the tester asked for them gone. The form's margins come from the body's side padding and the empty head and foot of `.ir-page`, which repeat on every sheet; the `<title>` names no learner. Only the F-DAR section may break across sheets (its column heads repeat); every other section keeps together.
- **The AI grammar and terms check** (`student-incidents.proofread`, `App\Support\ClinicalProofreader`) proposes corrected wording for Focus, Data, body part, findings, Action and Response in **one** Gemini request (same `GEMINI_API_KEY`; `GEMINI_TEXT_MODEL` names a different model to keep the check off the photo readers' daily allowance). **It writes nothing**: each suggestion is shown against what was written, word by word, to Accept or Keep Mine, and the report is saved through the one write path as typed. **The server drops a suggestion that changes any figure or loses a name placeholder**, and **the learner's, the witnesses' and the nurse's names are masked before the text leaves the server** and restored on the way back. Filers only, throttled (12/min), audited with field names and never the text. Without a key the button is not drawn and the endpoint answers 503.

**Leaving the Incident Reports tab, or coming back to it, closes the form** (a capture-phase `.sp-tab` click listener in the script): the nurse returns to the default view, never to a half-filed or half-edited report; a second click on the tab already open leaves the form alone. **While the form is open — filing or editing — the Incident History is hidden** (`#incidentHistory`, toggled with the form in the script's `setOpen()`), so the report being written is never confused with one already filed; Cancel or Save brings it back.

`IncidentReportFormTest` guards all three, beside `StudentIncidentReportTest`.

### Clinic Data Visualization (live, one reading)

`/dashboard/data-visualization` (`DataVisualizationController` → `dashboard/data-visualization.blade.php` + `dashboard/visualization/*` partials, `css/school-nurse-data-visualization.css`) is the nurse's, clinic teacher's and clinic staff's charts page. It used to be a sample layout with every figure typed into the markup; now **every chart is read from the live tables through `App\Support\ClinicVisualization`**, which computes nothing of its own: nutrition and feeding come from `SchoolHeadOverview`, clinic and inventory from `SchoolHeadHealthOverview` — the readings the head's tabs print — so the two roles cannot report different numbers for one school. It adds only chart geometry (shares, ring offsets, point positions). The head's rules hold: a learner nobody weighed is **Not measured**, shares are over the learners measured and an empty share is an em dash, the trend runs June → current month with empty months kept as zeros, the endline series is drawn only once someone is re-measured, and the outcome split is the shared `partials/outcome-split`. Aggregates only — no panel carries a learner's name.

One filter row scopes everything: school year (navigates — its roll decides the grade/section lists), grade and section (re-read the panels in place) and **Weighing** (Baseline / Latest / Endline — all three are rendered and the control only picks which is on screen, so the KPI tiles move with the charts). The page polls `workspace.pulse` and, only when the stamp moves or a filter changes, fetches `dashboard.data-visualization.data`, which renders the **same partials** (`DataVisualizationController::PANES`) the first paint used and swaps them in, dimming the old render meanwhile. What the reader chose off the query string — a card switched to its table view, a hidden trend series, the expanded medicine list — is re-applied after every swap. Every chart has a table twin. Colours are validated steps declared at the top of the page sheet (the `--sh-*` status set, the theme's series pair, a stock-state trio valid in worst-first order with a badge on every row, and `#007C99` for single-series counts); re-run the dataviz validator before changing any. `NurseDataVisualizationTest` guards it.


### RxNorm Allergy Check (RxNav API)

Every medicine dispensed on a consultation is checked against the learner's recorded medicine allergies through RxNav, the U.S. National Library of Medicine's free, keyless RxNorm API. Full reference: `docs/rxnorm-allergy-check.md`. Rules to preserve:

- **`App\Support\RxNormService` is the only code that calls RxNav**, and it is called only when a medicine or an allergy is **saved** — never at dispense time. Three endpoints: approximateTerm (name → candidates), related.json?tty=IN+MIN (the `IN` group only; `MIN` lists every product an ingredient is in) and rxclass byRxcui ATC (classes reached only through a combination product are dropped). Answers are cached in `rx_concepts` / `rx_concept_ingredients`. Only a medicine name is sent; logs carry the endpoint and status, never the term.
- **No API failure stops a save.** A medicine or allergy is stored `unverified` and `rxnorm:retry-unverified` (daily; `--all-learners` once to backfill) asks again. A top candidate is taken only when its name is the name asked for; otherwise the nurse chooses from three (`medicine-inventory.rxnorm`). Nothing is picked automatically. `config/rxnorm.php` maps PH names first (paracetamol → acetaminophen, mefenamic acid → mefenamate).
- **`App\Support\AllergyCheck` decides the result from the database only**, and the same `evaluate()` answers the dialog (`student-allergies.check`) and the save (`ConsultationController::store`), so the page is never the enforcement. Nurse: same ingredient = warning (override reason ≥ 10 characters), same ATC level-3 = caution, anything not matched = unverified (both need acknowledgement). Restricted roles (`DispensingRights`): any paracetamol allergy, matched or only named in the text, is blocked with no override. **An unverified allergy is never read as clear**, and no learner chosen off the roll is unverified. Every result is written to `dispense_allergy_checks` (blocked attempts too, with no consultation). A restricted role posting another medicine is refused with **403**.
- **`App\Support\MedicineAllergies` keeps `learner_medicine_allergies` in step** with what `LearnerAllergies` reads (Sheet 1, both consent forms, the health assessment), splitting a line into medicines. Sheet 1 asks Medicine or Food (`health_history.allergies_type`); a food allergy is never looked up, and a general one is. Every save that can change an allergy calls `MedicineAllergies::syncLater()` (deferred until after the response). The allergy text is encrypted; both tables are purged with the learner.
- `phpunit.xml` needs nothing for this: `tests/TestCase.php` calls `Http::preventStrayRequests()`, so a test that forgets to fake RxNav reads it as unavailable. `RxNormAllergyCheckTest` covers RX-01 to RX-15.

### Clinic records (from Key Models and Their Scope)

- `Consultation` — belongs to a `Condition`. Created by clinic staff/nurse during clinic visits. **Clinic Staff can correct one after it is saved** (`Consultation::EDIT_ROLES`, `consultations.update`, the Edit button on the Consultation Log and the Clinic Staff dashboard, `partials/consultation-edit-button`). It reopens the one consultation dialog in an edit mode rather than a second form. The medicine is not editable, since it is a stock movement with an allergy check behind it: it shows read-only, and a visit with a dispense keeps its learner, on the server as well as on the page. A changed condition follows onto the dispense's reason. Who recorded the visit stands, and the change goes in the audit trail with old and new values; a value is assigned only when it differs, because these columns encrypt on write and every assignment would otherwise read as a change. `ConsultationEditTest` guards it. **It keeps who recorded it** (`recorded_by_name`, encrypted; `recorded_by_role`, plain — migration `2026_11_03_000003`, which named the visits already on file from their audit entries), and `ConsultationMedicines::recordedBy()` prints it as **Recorded By** on the Consultation Log, the clinic staff dashboard and the profile's Consultation Log tab, falling back to whoever dispensed on the visit (`dispensed_by_name`) for one nobody could name. Clinic roles only; the adviser's redacted view never carries it.
- **Sheet 2 is one structure, read three ways.** `App\Support\Sheet2Review` defines the MLAT's Sheet 2 — F. the twelve body systems (finding + notes), G. vision/hearing, H. oral health, I. immunization, J. summary — as the clinic's workbook lays it out. The nurse **fills** it on Fill Medical Record (`nurse/examine.blade.php`, saved by `NurseController::saveExamination` under `examination['sheet2']` via `fromInput()`, signed by the session on the date of examination); the profile's **Sheet 2 tab** renders it (`renderSheet2` in `student-health-records.blade.php`) and the **MLAT download** writes it (`MlatWorkbook::sheetTwo`) — all through `read()`. Until the nurse has filled it, `read()` derives a first draft from the adviser's `systems_review` checklist and the older examination fields, and the form says so; once the nurse's sheet exists it is never back-filled from the adviser's. Never lay Sheet 2 out a second time in a view. **Sheet 2 is answered from lists, not typed.** Every field whose answer is one of a fixed set is a **dropdown**, as the clinic's template rules it: the body-system findings (`FINDINGS`, **one list per system** as the template's "Findings (Check / Status)" column has it — Eyes are Clear / Redness / Discharge, Respiratory Clear Breath Sounds / Cough / Wheezing, and so on; the first is the system's normal finding, read through `findingsFor()` / `normalFinding()` / `isAbnormal()`), the vision outcome (`VISION_RESULTS` — Pass / Refer), the hearing outcome (`HEARING_RESULTS` — Passed Both / Failed Right / Failed Left / Refer), the teeth condition (`TEETH` — Good / Fair / Poor / Dental Caries / Gum Inflammation / Missing/Broken Teeth), the dental referral (`DENTAL_REFERRALS`) and the immunization status (`IMMUNIZATION`). Only the two eye measurements, the last dental visit, the vaccines and the J. prose are typed. `fromInput()` runs every one through `option()` and drops anything not on its list, and the draft derived from the adviser's checklist lands **on an option** — an older free-text result is read onto the option it meant or left blank, and the referral is one of the two choices rather than a sentence (what the adviser ticked is already on the body-system rows). A sheet saved under the older Normal / Abnormal pair still reads (`legacySystems()`): Normal becomes the system's normal finding, and Abnormal stays a finding only where the system lists the word — elsewhere it moves into the notes, so nothing the nurse recorded is dropped.
- **Vital signs are recorded by the school nurse, clinic staff and the Clinic Teacher** (the Clinic Teacher admitted 2026-11-10; `StudentVitalSigns::WRITE_ROLES`, read by both `student-vitals.store` and the health records page, which draws Record Vital Signs only for those roles — never `hidden` on a `.btn`, whose own `display` outranks the attribute). Everyone else who opens the profile reads them. **A blood pressure is systolic/diastolic or nothing.** The Vital Signs panel (`student-vitals.store`) and the health assessment form both validate it through `App\Rules\BloodPressure`, which reads `StudentVitalSigns::parseBloodPressure()` / `bloodPressureProblem()`: two whole numbers either side of a slash, systolic 50–250 and diastolic 30–150 mmHg, systolic higher — so "normal", "110" or "70/110" never land on the record or the MLAT. It is stored as `118/76` (`normalizeBloodPressure()`). The panel's field strips anything that cannot be part of a reading as it is typed and checks the same limits before posting; the endpoint is the guarantee. `StudentVitalSignsRoleTest` guards it.
- **The MLAT downloads as the clinic's own workbook.** `App\Support\MlatWorkbook::build($record)` lays the Mandatory Learner's Health Assessment Tool out exactly as the school's spreadsheet has it — Sheet 1 (A. Learner Information → E. Vital Signs & Anthropometrics) and Sheet 2 (F. Body Systems → J. Summary and Recommendations), the same lettered bands, labels and cell positions — as a **model** (sheets → rows → cells + register, plus how a row's cells sit: `merge` across the whole row, `merges` across a run of columns, `ruled`, `wrap`; each sheet its own `widths` and `orientation`), and that one model has **two writers**: `MlatExportController::download` (`student-mlat.download`) writes it through OpenSpout, and `printSheet` (`student-mlat.print`, `dashboard/mlat-print.blade.php`) lays one sheet out for paper through `printRows()` — which mirrors the spreadsheet, letting text run on over empty cells where the file has no merge. Both are under `/health-records/*`, for nurse / clinic staff / clinic teacher / the learner's own adviser, and audited (`downloaded` / `printed`). Both profiles offer them as **Print ▾ / Download ▾ menus of `MlatWorkbook::SHEETS`** (`partials/mlat-menus` + `-script`), labelled **MLHAT 1 / MLHAT 2** — the same names the workbook's tabs and the one-sheet file names (`MLHAT-1-<learner>.xlsx`) carry: `?sheet=1|2` downloads a one-sheet workbook with that sheet's page setup, and Print loads the sheet into a hidden frame and prints the frame, so the profile stays open. Each sheet is written in **its own column widths** — they used to be set on the options, which made Sheet 1's every sheet's fallback. **Sheet 1's layout is the one the clinic approved; Sheet 2 carries the ruled F table (notes merged C–F) and wrapped I/J rows** — `sheet_one_keeps_its_layout` pins the first. **Every tick box on Sheet 1 is `[√]` / `[  ]` (`MlatWorkbook::checked()`) in a cell of its own** — Sex, each Medical History item including Allergies and Hospitalization/Surgery, each Family History item — because a run of "[ √ ] Male [   ] Female" in one cell only showed while the cell beside it happened to be empty, and was cut off wherever it was not. The mark is **U+221A**, the printed form's own "tsek (√)", because it is in Arial, the font those cells are written in, and in every common text font. Never ✓ (U+2713) or the ☐ / ☑ ballot boxes: none of them is in those fonts, so Excel borrows them from another font while WPS Office and tablet apps may draw nothing, and a ticked answer reads as blank. `MlatExportTest::the_tick_is_in_the_font_the_workbook_names` guards it. Keep the content in the model, never in a writer: it is what makes every cell testable without the library (`MlatExportTest`), and what makes the printed sheet and the file the same form. Every cell is read off the record — the adviser's Sheet 1/2, the nurse's vitals and examination, baseline and endline — and a reading nobody took is a blank or "None", never a plausible value; the nurse's one set of vitals is printed on the period it was recorded in and never repeated on the other row. **The lock file's OpenSpout 5.8 needs PHP ≥ 8.4** (so does Symfony 8, hence `"php": "^8.4"` in `composer.json`); on an older PHP every workbook-reading test skips or fails.
- **The New Consultation dialog shows the learner's recorded allergies before anything is handed over.** `App\Support\LearnerAllergies::for()` reads every place one is recorded — the adviser's health card (Sheet 1 `health_history`), the parent's Sulat-Pahibalo, a returned paper consent, the older health assessment — for every school year the learner has at this school, merges the same allergy from two places into one with both sources, treats a "wala" / "none" line as nothing (`ConsentFormScanner::saysSomething()`) and a ticked box with no detail as `Not specified`. It is served one learner at a time by `student-allergies.show` (`/health-records/students/{lrn}/allergies`, nurse / clinic staff / clinic teacher, scoped, audited) when the learner is chosen — never embedded for the whole roll — and the dialog flags a chosen medicine whose name a medicine allergy names. The learner picker matches an **LRN** as well as a name (digits anywhere in the LRN, spaces and dashes ignored, the LRN shown on each result) and refuses to file a visit under an LRN nobody was chosen for. `ConsultationAllergiesAndMedicineTest` guards it.
- **A clinic note reaches the class adviser only if the clinic sends it.** `consultations.notes` is clinical text and stops at the clinic like the complaint and the treatment (`ConsultationVisibility::DETAIL_ROLES` is unchanged — the adviser question in `docs/open-decisions.md` entry 1 is still open). What changed is that the nurse can now hand **one visit's note** over deliberately: `consultations.notes_shared_with_adviser` (plain boolean — a flag is not a fact about a child) is ticked beside the note on New Consultation. `ConsultationVisibility::sharedNote()` is the one test, and a shared note is **added to the redacted payload** (`shared_note`) rather than a template deciding to print something it was sent: an unshared note never reaches the browser. The adviser's Consultation Log renders it as `Note from the clinic`; nothing else about the visit widens. `ConsultationPrivacyTest` guards both halves. **Consultation photographs never reach the adviser at all** (since 2026-11-04): `ConsultationPhotoController` serves the list and the image to the clinic roles only, the per-photo "share with adviser" control and its endpoint are gone, the visit payload carries no photo count, and the `consultation_photos.shared_with_adviser` column is left in place and read by nothing. `ConsultationPhotoTest` guards it.
- `MedicineDispense` — one draw on stock. **There is one path into it and no page of its own:** `ConsultationController::store` writes the row and decrements the medicine inside a single transaction, holding a `lockForUpdate()` on the row so two nurses dispensing at once cannot both read the same stock level and drive it negative. The standalone Dispensing Log page and its `MedicineDispenseController` were retired — a second form for the same write is a second way for the two to disagree about what the school has left — and who may give which medicine is `DispensingRights`' answer, applied on the write. **A dispense is filed on its visit** (`medicine_dispenses.consultation_id`) and records **which lots it came out of** (`medicine_dispense_batches`, see `MedicineBatch` below), so `App\Support\ConsultationMedicines` prints "Paracetamol 500mg tablet × 2 tablets (exp 30 Apr 2027)" beside the visit on the Consultation Log, the nurse's / clinic teacher's / clinic staff's dashboards and the profile's Consultation Log tab — two queries for a whole list, clinic roles only (the adviser's redacted view never carries it). The free-text field beside it is **Recommendations** (still the `treatment_given` column): the medicine is a field of its own, not a sentence in the treatment. The learner's name, the reason and the dispenser are encrypted; `medicine_id`, `quantity`, `dispensed_at` and `institution_id` stay plain, which is what lets `App\Support\MedicineUsage` group consumption in SQL without ever touching who received it. **Nothing renders a per-learner dispensing history any more** — the rows are kept and audited, but no screen lists them; that is a consequence of removing the page, not an oversight. `MedicineDispenseTest` guards the record's invariants and `MedicineInventoryUsageTest` the consultation path.
- `MedicineReceipt` — the logbook's other column: one delivery **into** stock. **There is one path in, as there is one path out:** `MedicineInventoryController::receive` (`medicine-inventory.receive`, nurse and clinic staff, re-scoped to the school) writes the receipt row and the stock increment in one transaction under `lockForUpdate()`, the mirror of a dispense — before it existed a level could only be typed at creation and drawn down, so the first delivery put the record out of step with the shelf. The receiver's name is encrypted; quantity, dates, source and the medicine stay plain. **Expiry is judged per item off the earliest date on hand** (`medicines.expiry_date`, plain): a delivery onto an empty or already-expired shelf sets it, a delivery onto live stock keeps the sooner date, and a delivery already past its date is refused. `Medicine::expiryStatus()` / `statusOfDate()` is the one reading — expired / expiring (`EXPIRY_WARNING_DAYS`, 30) / ok / **NULL for no date on file**, which the page prints as an em dash and never as fine; an empty shelf is never counted as expiring. `MedicineExpiryAndReceiptTest` guards all of it. **An item is created once per school and received into after that**: Add Medicine refuses a name the school already stocks, compared without case, spacing or punctuation (`MedicineCatalogue::comparableName()`), with a pointer to that item's Receive Stock, and the dropdown marks a stocked catalogue item "(in inventory)" and disables it (the database's unique index on `medicines.name` is still global, not per school). **A supply is not a drug** (`MedicineCatalogue::isSupply()`: `config('medicines.supplies')`, or a typed name with a supply word): no generic name is asked for or looked up, its allergy-check badge reads "Not applicable", and the nightly RxNorm retry skips it — the dispense check still treats any unmatched item as one to check by hand. `config('medicines.generic_names')` gives the generic where the catalogue name does not say it (Antifungal cream → Clotrimazole). **The Predictive Reorder panel reads any item** (`?forecast=<id>`, the item nearest to running out by default): with dispensing in the last six months the target is the last three months' average × 1.2, never below the item's minimum threshold; with none, the target is the threshold itself, so a Low Stock item still recommends reaching it. `MedicineInventoryTesterFixesTest` guards these.
- `MedicineBatch` — **one lot on the shelf, and the answer to "which box do I hand over".** An item's stock is held in lots (`medicine_batches`): the stock it was created with (`Opening stock`, written by `store()`) and each delivery (written by `receive()`, linked to its receipt), every lot with its own expiry date and `quantity_remaining`, **always adding up to `stock_quantity`**. `App\Support\MedicineBatches` is the only thing that writes them, and holds three rules: **first expiry, first out** (a dispense draws from the usable lot that expires soonest — undated after every dated lot, the older delivery first on a tie — and moves to the next only when it runs out); **expired stock is never dispensed** (a lot is expired on its expiry date, as an item is; a draw larger than the usable stock is refused and rolls the visit back); and **the item's own date is read off the lots** (`refreshExpiry()`: the earliest usable lot, unknown when the usable stock carries no date, the expired date only once nothing usable is left — so the date moves on when the earliest lot is used up). Expired stock stays counted, because it is physically there, until a stock writer takes it off the shelf with **Remove expired** (`medicine-inventory.dispose-expired`, `disposeExpired()`: nurse and clinic staff, scoped, one transaction, the lot keeps `quantity_disposed` and who removed it, encrypted, audited). When the lots and the stock figure disagree (an item from before lots, a level set outside this class) the stock figure wins: unaccounted stock is an untracked lot at the item's date, and lots claiming more than the item holds are rebuilt as one — computed on read, persisted by `reconcile()` inside every write. The Medicine Inventory's **Dispense Next** column, the dialog's option labels (usable count and next expiry) and its **Batch** line all read `MedicineBatches::shelves()` (one query per page). Migration `2026_10_26_000001_track_medicine_batches` divides existing stock once: what is left is taken to be the latest-expiring deliveries, the rest is the opening stock at the item's date. Until it runs, `supported()` is false and stock is one figure exactly as before. `MedicineBatchesTest` guards all of it.
