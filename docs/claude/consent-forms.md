# Consent forms (Sulat-Pahibalo)

Moved word for word from CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-09. CLAUDE.md keeps the rules most tasks need; this file holds the full rules for parental consent: the Sulat-Pahibalo, the upload dialog, the photo reader, the paper recorder and the clinic consent list. Mentions of "the … section" may point to a section now in another file under docs/claude/.

### Consent records (from Key Models and Their Scope)

- `ParentalConsentForm` — one signed form a parent returned, per student per school year. `ParentalConsentForm::currentSchoolYear()` computes the year dynamically. Every upload is filed as `PROGRAM_HEALTH_SERVICES` (stored as `Deworming`, its name from when deworming was its only service), the Sulat-Pahibalo scan. `PROGRAM_FEEDING` (`SBFP`) rows from the retired feeding-consent gate may still be in the table, so every reader of the health-services answer goes through `healthServices()` (or the `Deworming` filter the older readers carry) — an SBFP consent is not permission for deworming. **A partial consent's exceptions can be recorded as services** (`excluded_services`, encrypted, migration `2026_11_03_000002`): `ConsentStanding::answer()` returns them as `excluded` for an upload, so those services read Declined instead of "Check the exceptions"; NULL (every older row, and any upload not confirmed) keeps the old reading.
- **A consent that came back on paper has one way in, and a human at the end of it.** Most consents arrive through the parent's own link; the consent half of the document is editable only there (`$editConsent = ($mode === 'parent')`), so a form signed in ink had no path into the system at all. `HealthConsentFormController::paperForm` / `recordPaperForm` (`consent-forms.paper`, `consent-forms.paper.store`, adviser-only, scoped, audited as *Recorded from the signed paper form*) is that path. **The reader proposes; the adviser records.** `App\Support\ConsentFormScanner` reads a photograph of the Sulat-Pahibalo with **Gemini vision** (`GEMINI_API_KEY`, the same REST call, retry and model as the masterlist reader; the prompt carries the whole printed form in Cebuano with its meaning and the words parents write on it, and tells the model to recognise Cebuano handwriting, never translate it) — **whether the parent agreed — and which services they left out — is read from their own reply under "TIMAAN SA PAGDAWAT…" alone**, which the response schema returns grouped as `parent_reply` (the three consent boxes `CONSENT_BOXES`, the handwritten lines, the services the "gawas lamang niini" line names as `excluded_services`, the allergy section, the name and `signature_present`; the date is the header's PETSA line, `form_date`, since the reply has no date of its own). **The school's list of services at the top is the school's offer and is not read at all** (set 2026-11-10, testers' report #2: an unshaded "Check Up sa Dentist" there had been read as not consented): the schema has no field for it and the prompt says an empty box there is not a refusal; the model describes each box in a few words first (`consent_evidence`, ordered before the verdicts, shown to the adviser beside an UNCLEAR reason and never ruled on), compares the three against each other rather than against white paper, then reports each box separately and `decideConsent()` rules on them (exactly one ticked and the other two clearly empty is an answer; none, two, an unreadable box, a self-contradicting summary or writing on another box's line is UNCLEAR with a `consent_reason` the adviser is shown) — **each allergy row on its own box** (the rows sit close together and a tick's tail crosses the box above it, so a row is found by its printed words, a tick belongs to the row its bottom point is in, and the reader describes every box before answering — `consent_evidence`, `allergy_evidence` — and the image goes at `services.gemini.media_resolution`, default `MEDIA_RESOLUTION_ULTRA_HIGH`, since a model gives any image the same tokens whatever its size; blank `GEMINI_MEDIA_RESOLUTION` for a model that refuses the level; **because of that same limit both pages also send close-ups** (`consent-forms._photo-closeups`, up to four overlapping bands cut in the browser because the server has no image library, posted as `closeups[]` after the whole sheet and read, never kept), which took the reader from miscounting a tick's tail as a tick on the row above to reading those rows correctly, and a request without them is still read whole; **a sheet is read upright or not at all**: on a sideways photo the reader put ticks confidently on the wrong rows, so it reports `sheet_orientation` first, a sheet not upright proposes nothing and returns `turn` (`TURN_TO_UPRIGHT`, degrees clockwise), and both pages turn the photo in the browser and read it once more, never a third time. (The hold-back of service rows on a photo wider than it is tall went with the service list.) The browser also sends `sheet`, the whole photo drawn upright with a phone's rotation tag applied, which the reader gets in place of `photo`; `photo` stays the adviser's own file, the one filed and the one `scan_token` is tied to) — and through `WRITTEN_FIELDS` the handwritten blanks (excepted services, refusal reason, the three allergy lines, other illness) each with a `confident` flag — and `interpret()` is where the safety rules live: **a reading that leaves out a consent box, or answers one off the list, is not used at all** — no partial result; the draft is unreadable with `incomplete_reading` and `READING_INCOMPLETE`, and the adviser fills the form in from the paper (set 2026-11-09) — an unreadable photo answers nothing at all, a blank read without confidence keeps its text **and** is named in `unclear`, a blank nobody could make out at all reads **`UNREADABLE_TEXT` ("Unreadable")** rather than arriving empty — an empty field is the claim that the parent wrote nothing, which is a different thing to say about a child's allergies and cannot be told apart from the real answer once the form is saved, while `readable` defaults to true so a model that omits the key does not turn every blank into "Unreadable", and `needs_review` is decided once there so no screen reaches its own verdict. `consent-forms.scan` still **writes nothing**; `recordPaperForm` reads its values from the submitted form, refuses without the photograph and without the adviser's `confirmed` attestation, and refuses a form that already carries an answer. The photograph is the evidence — a paper consent has no drawn-PNG signature — so it is stored through `EncryptedFileStorage`, its plain path kept on `health_consent_forms.paper_form_path`, and served by `consent-forms.paper.image` only to the roles that may already open the form. **Whoever acts on the answers can see the paper they came from:** the read-only form page the nurse, clinic staff, clinic teacher and School Head open (`nurse-show`) links the photograph and says the answers were entered by the adviser. The image route therefore lives under the shared `/dashboard/consent-forms/…` path (the head reads it as `consent-forms.head-paper-image` under their own), never under `/dashboard/class-adviser/…`, where `EnsureActiveSession` would turn a demo nurse into the adviser. **The same reader proposes an answer on the Upload Sulat-Pahibalo dialog too** (`parental_consent_forms`, the other way a consent reaches the nurse): "Read the answer from the photo" posts the attached JPG/PNG to `consent-forms.scan` and sets the dialog's `consent_type` select, an UNCLEAR reading leaves it on *Not recorded yet* with `consent_reason` shown, and a reader-supplied answer is filed only with the adviser's `answer_confirmed` tick (`answer_from_scan` → `accepted_if`, refused server-side) and an audit entry saying so. Choosing the answer by hand stays exactly as it was. Three more readings ride on the same draft, each only ever able to make the adviser look again: a **"wala" / "N/A" / "-" on another box's line says nothing** (`saysSomething()`, so it never makes a clear answer UNCLEAR, and the parent's word is kept as written); the **allergy and illness boxes (`ALLERGY_BOXES`) are read beside their lines**, so a ticked box over an empty line is filed as ticked and read as `LearnerAllergies::NOT_SPECIFIED` (both consent pages fill "Not specified" into the field) instead of being saved as "no allergy", **and is put to the adviser as `ConsentFormScanner::BOX_TICKED_NO_DETAILS`** ("Box ticked but no details written — check with the parent.", a field note and a check; the allergy heading shaded with nothing ticked or written under it says the same), while a ticked box over a line reading "wala" lands in `field_notes`, and an "except" with nothing excepted or a refusal with no reason is put to the adviser the same way; and **the learner's name is read off the sheet and checked** — the scan takes an optional `lrn`, the controller resolves it only within the adviser's own class roster, and `checkLearner()` turns a sheet naming another child (`namesAgree()`: order-free, initials ignored, both surname and a given name required, so a sibling's sheet fails) into `learner_mismatch` with no consent proposed and nothing filled in, and a name it could not read at all into a check asking the adviser to make sure the sheet is that learner's. The model's `is_sulat_pahibalo` verdict, like the masterlist reader's, can only refuse. **A sheet dated in another school year is named** (testers filed one dated 27 October 2024 under S.Y. 2027–2028): the reader reports the PETSA line as `form_date` — the words, and year / month / day only where read for certain, 0 otherwise — and `checkSchoolYear()` rules on it against the year the page is filing under (`school_year` on the scan request; the upload dialog sends its own year, the paper recorder the form's, the current year when neither does), June to May. Outside it, `date_warning` opens with `DATE_OUTSIDE_SCHOOL_YEAR` ("This form is dated outside the selected school year.") and says what the PETSA reads; it leads the reading's checks on both pages and is repeated on Confirm Upload. It decides nothing: the answer is still proposed and still the adviser's to file. A year with no month is outside only when no month could put it inside, and a date nobody could read raises nothing. `ConsentFormScanTest` guards the reader, `ConsentPaperFormTest` the flow, `ConsentAnswerCaptureTest` the upload.
- **The clinic's consent list is the year's roll, read through one status function** (`consent-forms.nurse-index` → `App\Support\NurseConsentList`, read by the nurse, clinic staff and clinic teacher, each named as themselves). **`ConsentStanding::answer()` is the one reading of which record answers** (an answered letter, else a scan whose answer was keyed in) and `for()` now goes through it; **`forService()` decides a learner's standing for one service in a fixed order** — ruled out for the learner (key `not_applicable`, shown as **Not offered**, as the School Nurse confirmed on 2026-11-09; `ConsentStanding::appliesTo()`: `GRADE_ONLY_SERVICES` Grade 7 MR/Td, `SEX_ONLY_SERVICES` Iron for female learners — coded, because they hold in every school) → Not offered by the school (`HealthServicesOffered`, below) → Not enrolled → No form yet → Not offered (not shaded at the top, when the form's list is known) → Declined → **Not offered** (key `not_on_form`: not shaded at the top of the form, or not offered by the school that school year — never a refusal, so a refusal declines only what its form listed) → *Check the exceptions* (a "yes, except" whose exceptions are only the parent's handwriting — never guessed) → Consented; `overall()` answers for every service. The nine services are `HealthConsentForm::PLANNED_SERVICES` (on a saved letter a ticked heading covers its sub-items; on a photographed sheet a row counts on its own box alone, `ConsentPaperCopy::tickedServices()`). Service, grade and year are scope and move the four cards; the status filter narrows the list alone; a service ruled out for the learner, Not enrolled and Not offered are in no count, and a rate over nobody is an em dash. **The name search runs in the browser** over the rows on the page, because `AuditTrail` stores the full URL and a name in a query string would land in the audit log. **No full LRN is printed** — rows carry `maskLrn()`'s last five digits, and View opens `consent-forms.nurse-learner`, keyed by record id, which shows the standing, the photo (through the existing role-checked image routes, never a public URL), the values the adviser confirmed with any required field missing, and who filed it; it never calls the photo reader and writes nothing. (The topbar learner search still embeds full LRNs — an open decision.) `config('app.simulated_data')` (`SIMULATED_DATA`, off) shows a testing banner and watermarks the photo, and must stay off wherever real learners are on file. `NurseConsentListTest` guards it.
- **The adviser's View Details is the clinic's reading of the same form.** `NurseConsentList::reading()` (the query-free half of `learner()`) renders each learner's panel on the adviser's Parent's Consent page into a `<template class="pc-view-source">`, cloned in on open so the photo is fetched through `parental-consent.download` only when somebody looks: the uploaded photo (a PDF in a frame) beside **the form drawn back as the paper carries it** — the Sulat-Pahibalo's own Cebuano wording in its own order: each planned service as a row under its printed name (Pag Purga and Immunization heading their sub-items) with its standing pill (`forService()`, or an amber `?` when the paper has to be read), under three section heads (Services, Parent's consent, Allergies and illnesses), every service row carrying the teal "+" service marker so it is never mistaken for one of the parent's boxes, every consent and allergy box as a row of the same kind with a Ticked / Not ticked / amber `?` pill, and every handwritten line as written inside the row of the box it belongs to, never translated or summarised — and who filed it. There is no separate English per-service list any more; the rows are it. `App\Support\ConsentPaperCopy` builds that copy from an upload, an answered letter or the photo reader's draft, and `consent-forms._paper-copy` draws it: View Details, the clinic's learner page, the Upload dialog right after "Read the answer from the photo" (`consent-forms.scan` returns it as `copy_html`) and the paper recorder all show the one drawing. The printed consent and allergy wording lives once on `HealthConsentForm::CONSENT_TEXT` / `ALLERGY_TEXT`, which the online letter reads too. An upload records **the services ticked on the paper** (`parental_consent_forms.services`, encrypted, migration `2026_10_28_000001`) and its write-in lines; an upload with none reads as the program it was filed for, as every older upload does. The dialog no longer lists the services to tick: the photo reader reads them off the paper and posts them as hidden `services[]` fields, and once it has read a photo the dialog widens to show **the photo beside the reading** — the same `.pc-view-*` layout as View Details (Uploaded form + Enlarge on the left; Read from the uploaded photo, a complete / check / unreadable line from the scan's `summary`, and the paper copy on the right). An upload filed without a reading carries no services and reads as the program it was filed for. The reader still fills the write-in lines as a proposal. `AdviserConsentPageTest` guards it.
- **The Upload Sulat-Pahibalo dialog is one partial, opened from two pages** (`consent-forms._upload-modal`, markup and script together; any `[data-consent-upload]` control opens it). The Consent Forms page passes the class (`uploadStudents`, a student list, a `data-lrn` picking one); a learner's own profile opens it from its **Consent tab** with that learner fixed (`uploadLearner`, a hidden `lrn`, no list). Both post to the one write path, `parental-consent.store`, which re-checks the class, and the store's `back()` returns the adviser to whichever page it came from — the profile reopens its Consent tab on `consent_success` or an error, since a redirect carries no `#tab=`. Never copy the dialog into a page: `AdviserConsentPageTest` asserts the two renders are identical below the student field. **The upload flow (set 2026-11-08): read → correct in the panel → tick → read-only summary → "Consent saved successfully."** (1) The photo reader pre-fills the reading beside the photo. (2) The adviser corrects it against the paper **in the reading’s Services section**, where every planned service for the class’s grade is a row with a **Consented / Excluded** toggle — a service the parent consented to that the reader missed is switched to Consented, one the parent excluded that the reader included is switched to Excluded — **worded as the printed letter words it, in Cebuano, never translated** (`HealthConsentForm::printedService()`: the service’s own line and the heading it is indented under on the paper, Pag Purga or Immunization — the Consent tab’s lists read the same); the allergy and illness lines are corrected in their own fields. With no reading (no reader set up, a PDF, an unreadable photo) the same toggles sit under the parent’s answer (`#pcServicesField`), shown for an agreed answer only. (3) "I checked this answer against the signed form" sits beside Upload Consent, **shown and required whenever a reading is on screen** (the server still requires it only for a reader’s answer, `answer_from_scan`). (4) Upload Consent opens **Confirm Upload, a read-only summary** (`#pcConfirmModal`, stacked above the dialog; no input of any kind — `AdviserConsentPageTest` pins it): the learner, the answer, the exception or reason, the medical certificate and notes when given, every service as Consented / Excluded (Declined on a refusal, Pending with no answer), and the four allergy and illness lines. **Back to Edit** returns to the dialog with every mark kept; **Confirm & Upload** files exactly what it shows. (5) The page comes back with **"Consent saved successfully."** **Each service is one of three, never two**: **Consented**, **Excluded** or **Not offered**, and which services there are is the system's, never the reader's (see *System logic* below — the shading at the top of the paper no longer decides anything). A full consent offers only Consented / Not offered, a partial all three (an Excluded mark asks again if the answer is switched to full). **Each row starts from the parent's answer** (`ConsentPaperCopy::proposal()`, served as `service_proposal` keyed `full` / `partial` / `refused`; the dialog applies the same rule with no reading), and **Mark all Consented** / **Mark all Not offered** is there for the adviser reading the paper by hand. **Rows the learner is never given are locked** with the reason under them: a service the school does not offer that year reads Not offered, and so does Iron for a male learner (the dialog carries each row's `offered` and `only_sex`, and each learner's `data-sex`); MR, Td is listed for every grade and locked to Not offered outside Grade 7 (`only_grade`, against the class's grade). **A box ticked on the online letter that the rules or the setting rule out is named** beside it while it is ticked (`ConsentStanding::boxConflict()`) — "Shaded, but it is given to female learners only and this learner is male. It is read as Not offered." — a warning, never a stop. The photo reading no longer reads the printed list, so it names no shading. The learner's Consent tab lists **Not Offered** apart from Not Approved, a service ruled out for the learner (another grade, the other sex) included. The adviser’s own marks override the reading and survive a switch between full and partial; they go with the photo or the learner. **A heading is its rows the school offers**: at Sta. Ana, Pag Purga is Pag Bitok / Worms, the only deworming offered, and nothing asks for a box under it — the old "Ticked, but none of the boxes under it is…" notice (`rowsRequiredNotice()` / `headingsWithoutRows()`) is gone from the reading, the dialog and the online letter, whose Send no longer refuses it. (`tickedServices()` / `unsettledServices()`, which read the printed list's shading, were removed on 2026-11-10; a reading files no services of its own, and an upload with no answer recorded reads as the program it is filed for.) Those rows, a box the reader could not settle, and every listed row of a "yes, except" whose line cannot be named as services (`exceptedServices()`: an explicit list of names only, Cebuano included, and a sentence or an unsure reading is never one) read **Check the paper**, with neither option chosen, and **Upload Consent waits until each is decided** (and until an agreed form has at least one Consented). A service the parent’s line does name starts Excluded. Every doubt lands on not approved, because a consent nobody looked at is an authorisation nobody gave. Confirm & Upload posts the Consented keys as `services`, the Excluded rows of the letter’s list as `excepted_services` on a partial answer, and `services_confirmed=1` — and the server refuses a confirmed agreed form with no service. A declined or unrecorded answer lists the services read-only and files exactly as before. **The learner's Consent tab then lists what the form approves** — Approved / Not Approved (and Check the Exceptions for an unresolved "yes, except") from `consent_detail.services`, which `StudentHealthRecordController::consentServices()` builds through `ConsentStanding::forService()`, the clinic's own rule, so the tab and the nurse's list cannot disagree.
- **System logic for the photo reader (set 2026-11-09, the team's spec).** The system, not the reader, decides which services appear and what is saved; each service ends **Consented**, **Excluded** or **Not offered**. (1) **The service list** is the services the school offers that school year (`HealthServicesOffered`, `ConsentPaperCopy::givenServices()`) with the School Nurse's rules applied per learner (`ConsentStanding::appliesTo()`: Grade 7 MR, Td for Grade 7 only, Iron for female learners only) — anything else is Not offered. **The school's list printed at the top of the paper is not read** (since 2026-11-10; it had been read and drawn back, and a Dentist row left unshaded there read "Not consented" — testers' report #2). (2) The reader is the existing tuned Gemini prompt; the spec's answer fields are derived from it in `interpret()`, not asked of the model: `answer` (`full` / `partial` / `refused` / `none_marked` / `multiple_marked`, or `unclear`), `parent_name_present` (a name at the signature line, read or not), `form_complete` (name and signature), `unmatched_exceptions` (a "yes, except" line `exceptedServices()` cannot name as services) and `unclear_fields` (field keys: an unreadable or unsure line, a line with a field note, `parent_guardian_name`, `consent_answer` when no box decided it). (3) **Validation:** every consent box must come back with an allowed value, or the reading is not used (above); `excluded_services` keeps only keys the letter has, and only under a "yes, except" whose line says something. (4) **Applying the answer** (`proposal()`): full → every service on the list Consented; partial → the services the parent's line names Excluded (`ConsentPaperCopy::excepted()`: the line's own words when they match the list exactly, else the services the reader named off that same line), the rest Consented, the exceptions shown exactly as written; a line that does not match word for word is highlighted and the reading says what it was read as (with nothing named, every row starts Consented); refused → every service Excluded (the dialog says Excluded; the clinic list still words a refused service Declined), with the reason shown; none or more than one marked → `consent_reason` opens with `ANSWER_UNDETERMINED` ("The parent's answer could not be determined."), the answer select is highlighted and the adviser chooses it, and the rows then start from that choice. A field in `unclear_fields`, a field with a note, and an unmatched exceptions line are highlighted (`.pc-input.is-check`, amber) until the adviser types in it. (5) The allergy and illness lines pre-fill the Food, Medicine, Previous immunization and Other illnesses fields, editable. (6) Nothing is saved from the reader alone: the dialog now posts the sealed `scan_token`, and **the store accepts an answer the adviser corrected** (it used to refuse one that differed from the reading); `answer_from_scan` is true only when the answer filed is the reader's, and a correction is audited as *Consent answer chosen by the class adviser in place of the photo reading*. **Missing parent name or signature** (`FORM_INCOMPLETE`): the reading says so, the dialog shows it under the "The parent's name and signature are on the form." tick (pre-filled from `form_complete`, always posted as `form_complete`; a request without the field files as complete, as before), and an upload filed unticked is saved with `parental_consent_forms.incomplete` (encrypted) — its answer is kept, but `ConsentStanding::for()` reads it PENDING, `forService()` / `overall()` return `SERVICE_INCOMPLETE` ("Incomplete form"), `badge()` returns `INCOMPLETE`, the deworming gate in `NurseController` refuses it, and the clinic list, nurse index and profile say Incomplete — until a complete form is on file. (7) **The review log** (`App\Support\ConsentReviewLog`, stored encrypted on `parental_consent_forms.scan_review`, migration `2026_11_09_000002`): for each service and each field (`consent_type`, `partial_exception`, `refused_reason`, the four allergy and illness lines, `form_complete`), `vlm` — what the dialog was pre-filled with — beside `final` — what was saved — and `edited`, with `edited` / `services_edited` / `fields_edited` totals; a value the reader did not propose is `null` and never counts as an edit. `ConsentPhotoUploadTest`, `ConsentFormScanTest` and `ConsentPhotoSummaryTest` guard it.
- **The adviser review flow (set 2026-11-09, the team's spec).** The adviser edits in one place: the "Read from the uploaded photo" panel beside the photo. While a reading is on screen the dialog *moves* its own fields into that panel (`dockFields()` / `undockFields()` in `consent-forms._upload-modal`): the parent's answer (Full consent / Partial consent / Refused), the excepted services, the refusal reason and the name-and-signature tick under *Parent's consent*; the four allergy and illness lines and the medical certificate under *Allergies and illnesses*; the service rows under *Services*. They go back to the form when the reading goes, so nothing is entered twice. Reading says "Reading consent form… This may take a few seconds." **Every row of an agreed form offers Consented / Excluded / Not offered** — the only three words for a service anywhere (`ConsentStanding::SERVICE_LABELS` reads a declined service *Excluded*; "Check the paper", "Not approved" and "Not consented" are gone; *Check the exceptions* remains only for an online letter's or an older upload's "yes, except" nobody has marked) — and marking a row Excluded under a full consent switches the answer to Partial. A partial consent whose exceptions cannot be named starts every row Consented with the line highlighted; Upload Consent and the server (`excepted_services` required on a confirmed partial) refuse a partial consent with no row Excluded. **"Services the parent excepted" is not typed**: it lists the rows marked Excluded, what the parent wrote is shown as written (`#pcExceptWritten`, highlighted when unmatched), and `partial_exception` files that writing, or with nothing read the Excluded rows' printed names. Any row or field changed from what the reading pre-filled says **"Edited"** (`snapshotReading()`), and clears when it matches again. **Upload Consent stays disabled until "I checked this answer against the signed form." is ticked.** Confirm Upload is read-only: learner, LRN, school year, file, answer, signature, **the Consented and the Excluded services as two lists**, allergies and illnesses. **Every service is named in full as printed** (`HealthConsentForm::printedName()`, `printedService()['full']`: a row with the heading it sits under, "Pag Purga / Pagpainom ug Tambal sa: Pag Bitok / Worms") in the panel, the summary, the reading's checks and the clinic's learner page. **A learner's consent status reads Valid Consent (full or partial), Refused or Incomplete** (`ConsentStanding::LABELS`, the adviser's list, the profile, the nurse's page); the learner's Consent tab lists Consented / Excluded / Not offered. `AdviserConsentPageTest` guards the dialog.
- **A PETSA no year can be read off is noted softly (testers' report #31, 2026-11-10):** `checkSchoolYear()` sets `date_note` to `ConsentFormScanner::DATE_UNREAD` ("Date could not be read; please check the form."), shown as a check under Date (PETSA); it decides nothing. The date read is kept with the upload in its `scan_result` (`form_date`). `ConsentFormScanTest::a_date_that_could_not_be_read_is_noted_softly`.
