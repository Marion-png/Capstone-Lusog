# School Head

Moved word for word from CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-09. CLAUDE.md keeps the rules most tasks need; this file holds the full rules for the School Head role. Mentions of "the … section" may point to a section now in another file under docs/claude/.

### School Head role (read, decide, export)

**The head reads; other roles write (invariant).** The role reviews, monitors and exports — it never encodes a measurement, adds or transfers a learner, marks a feeding day, touches inventory or edits an account, and since Approve / Return / Lock were removed it records no decision either. That is enforced server-side by `App\Http\Middleware\RestrictSchoolHeadWrites` (last in the `web` group, so a refused write is still audited first): **every** POST/PUT/PATCH/DELETE is refused with a 403 for `school_head` unless its route name is in `ALLOWED_ROUTES`. It is deny-by-default on purpose — a new write route anywhere in the app is refused for this role until somebody adds it deliberately, so the failure mode of forgetting is a head who cannot do something, never a head who silently overwrote a measurement.

The list holds **one** kind of route: how a person gets into or out of the app — `login.submit`, `logout`, `admin.login.submit`, `account.request.submit`. A session is the browser's own, not school data, and *the way out of a role must never be gated on the role*: a session still reading `school_head` (a real head who never signed out, or the demo session `EnsureActiveSession` seeds on any `/dashboard/school-head` URL) would otherwise refuse the very form that replaces it, so signing in as any other role failed with the read-only message. The role's one write over school data — recording a decision on a report — was removed with the Approve / Return / Lock buttons, so **the School Head now writes nothing at all beyond their own session**. Nothing touching a learner's measurement, enrolment, attendance, inventory or an approved account belongs on this list.

**Every write route must carry a name.** An unnamed POST reports its name as `null`, matches nothing on the list, and is refused whatever the list says — which is precisely how signing in broke: `POST /login` was unnamed and the `login` entry named only the GET form. `SchoolHeadRoleTest` fails the build on any unnamed write route, and pins that all six roles plus the System Admin can sign in over a stale head session. (The prototype auto-session is not an exception: `EnsureActiveSession` re-seeds a *demo* session for whichever role a URL belongs to, so a demo head on a coordinator URL has already become a demo coordinator before this middleware runs. A real account keeps its role and is refused — `SchoolHeadRoleTest` pins both.)

**Two readings behind every tab.** `App\Support\SchoolHeadOverview::for($institutionId, $schoolYear)` is the single source for the roster, the feeding programme and the nutritional picture (Dashboard, Feeding Program, Reports, Masterlist), so the Dashboard's undernourishment figure and the Reports tab's cannot drift apart. **Its scope is required, not optional:** without an `active_institution_id` it reads nothing rather than falling through to every school's children, which is what makes the adviser-to-head handover exact — an adviser's entry reaches this head because the adviser stamped their own `institution_id` on it, and a neighbouring school's can never arrive by accident (`InstitutionScope` ejects a scoped role with no school; this is the second lock on the same door). It re-decides nothing the programme already decides: beneficiary from `FeedingBeneficiarySummary::isBeneficiary()`, covered grades from `GRADE_LEVELS`, at-risk (flagged, counted only — the head is never handed the names) from `FeedingAttendanceFlag`, the cycle from `FeedingProgramCycle`, improvement from `FeedingNutritionProgress`. Two rules it adds, both about honesty of measurement:
   - **A learner nobody measured is "Not measured", never Normal.** The coordinator's `FeedingBeneficiarySummary::statusOf()` folds an unrecorded status into Normal so its four cards sum to the beneficiary total; that is wrong for a monitoring report, where "412 Normal" must not include 60 children nobody weighed. `phaseStatus()` returns `''` for an absent reading, `toScale()` returns `''` for anything the classifier does not emit, and every breakdown carries a separate `NOT_MEASURED` bucket. Shares are taken over the learners **measured**, and are NULL — an em dash — when none was: "0% undernourished" over a grade nobody weighed reads as good news about children no one has looked at.
   - **A rate with nothing behind it is undefined, not 0%.** The rehabilitation rate's denominator is every beneficiary (so it cannot creep up as few endlines arrive) but it stays NULL until at least one endline exists. Rehabilitation (`Normal` at endline) and improvement (climbed the wasting scale) are counted separately and never merged.

`App\Support\SchoolHeadHealthOverview::for($institutionId, $schoolYear, $records, $scopedRoster)` is the second: the **clinic, consent and inventory** half, read once and shared by the Dashboard, the Health Overview, Consent Compliance and Inventory tabs for the same reason. It keeps the same two rules (an undefined rate is an em dash; nothing is stored) and adds three of its own:
   - **The period is the school year**, so a head reading last year's oversight never sees this month's clinic traffic folded into it (`periodFor()` — June through May).
   - **Consent is valid only when a parent answered and did not refuse.** Four standings — `valid` / `awaiting` / `declined` / `none` — and a draft nobody sent counts as `none`, because reporting the school compliant on the strength of an unsent letter is the same error as reporting an unweighed learner as Normal. `status` is plain and filters in SQL; `consent_choice` is encrypted and is read after fetch.
   - **The reorder line is each medicine's own `minimum_threshold`**, never a constant here: out / low / monitor / good are all derived from what the clinic set for that item.
   Every grouping runs in PHP — a consultation's learner, grade and complaint are all encrypted at rest, so only `consulted_at`, `status`, `condition_id` and `institution_id` are ever named in a WHERE. `SchoolHeadOversightTest` guards it.
1. **The Dashboard is an oversight and decision-support screen, not an encoding workspace** (`SchoolHeadController`). It answers three questions and nothing else: what is happening in the clinic and the feeding programme, what needs management attention, and whether the school is meeting its health-programme obligations. In order — **the cards first, the filters under them**, because the figures are what the screen is opened for and a row of controls above them sits between the reader and the reading: the executive summary (eight school-level KPIs across every programme — roll, consultations, referrals, beneficiaries, attendance, at-risk, consent completion, stock needing attention), the **Attention required** queue, health-programme performance beside the clinic overview, the feeding programme, the nutritional assessment, consent beside inventory, and the programme overview beside the grade snapshot. **Every queue item is generated from a live condition** — at-risk beneficiaries, learners without valid consent, medicines out of or below their reorder line, unconfirmed scanned marks, today's unrecorded session, feeding days under `SchoolHeadOverview::FULL_TURNOUT_PERCENT`, learners awaiting enrolment, beneficiaries with no baseline, clinic referrals this month, a missing or upcoming endline — never a stored to-do, so an item disappears the moment its condition stops being true. The empty state is a real answer naming where the cycle stands, never a blank box. Eleven panels are `live-pane`s re-rendered from the same Blade partials the first paint used, off the existing pulse (`dashboard.school-head.metrics.pulse`, exempt in `AuditSensitiveAccess`); the refresh **forwards the page's own query string**, or a filtered dashboard would silently redraw itself as the whole school. The audit trail is deliberately **not** in the pulse stamp: every authenticated page view writes to it, so watching it would rebuild the dashboard on every poll. **The head reads what other roles write, so every tab watches for it** — through the app-wide mechanism described under *Cross-role live refresh*, not one of its own. `App\Support\SchoolPulse` (was `SchoolHeadPulse`; it was never the head's) stamps the school's whole write surface, the Dashboard re-renders its panels from it, and Health Overview, Consent Compliance, Inventory and Feeding Program include `partials/workspace-live`. Reports and Masterlist deliberately do not: a reload there would discard a half-typed review remark or the head's search and sort.
1a. **Three filters, and they move every panel together.** School year, grade and section (`sectionOptions()` offers every grade and section on the *whole* roll, so one choice never hides the next). Only the school year is a SQL filter; grade and section are applied in PHP through `SchoolHeadOverview::scopedTo()`, which reuses the marks, rule and cycle already fetched — a filtered dashboard costs no second query and cannot judge a learner by a different threshold than the unfiltered one did. A **narrowed roster narrows the clinic figures with it** (`$scopedRoster`): a consultation is matched to a learner by name, the only link the clinic log carries, and a visit matching nobody on a narrowed roll is left out. Unscoped, every visit counts, so a mistyped name can never drop one from the school-wide total. An unrecognised filter value is dropped rather than emptying the page. **With no year asked for, every head tab opens on the current school year** (`SchoolHeadOverview::defaultYear()`), never the newest year on record: testers found every tab on 2028–2029 because the school-year testing aid had filed a row there. A future year stays on the list to choose.
2. **The Feeding Program tab reads the marks and offers no way to change one** (`SchoolHeadProgramController`) — a second write path into `feeding_attendances` is how two screens start disagreeing about whether a child was fed. Three readings, no more: the 120-cell grid keyed to **recorded sessions, not calendar days**, the cycle's statistics, and the diverging status chart that aligns every grade on the Wasted/Normal boundary with one shared scale and a Baseline/Latest toggle moving the chart and its callout together. There is **no activity log** — it was removed; the audit trail is the System Admin's screen (`/dashboard/system-admin/audit-logs`), and a second rendering of it here was history without a job.
3. **Reports carries the programme's two charts, and both refuse to draw a reading nobody took.** `buildShiftChart()` is baseline against endline per category on the theme's validated `--series-risk` / `--series-healthy` pair — the same anatomy as the coordinator's Nutritional Progress panel (`.np-*`), so one comparison reads one way product-wide: colour carries *which weighing*, the row label carries the status, every bar is direct-labelled, 2px of surface separates the pair. **The endline series is drawn only once an endline exists** — a row of zeros would read as "every learner left this category", the opposite of "nobody has been re-measured". `buildTurnoutChart()` is turnout month by month, oldest column first, on a **fixed 0–100 axis** (a percentage's scale is not the data's to choose) with `FULL_TURNOUT_PERCENT` drawn across it as a dashed rule, so the gap is the reading; a month whose marks are all unconfirmed draws nothing rather than a zero, and a column below the line changes fill to the theme's generic risk series — never to one of the five `--sh-*` status colours, which mean a child's status and nothing else. Bar widths read against a clean axis (`axisMax()` steps 1/2/5 × 10ⁿ) so a count never lands between two gridlines.
4. **Reports carries two actions — View and Export — and the role writes nothing** (`SchoolHeadReportsController`). Approve / Return for correction / Lock are gone, and with them the last write this role had, so the invariant now reads exactly as it is stated: the head reads, monitors and exports; every other role writes. The `ReportReview` model and its `report_reviews` table are still in the tree but orphaned — nothing reads or writes them, and `SchoolHeadRoleTest` asserts the table stays empty; do not build on them. The reports themselves are **derived at read time and never stored**.

   **View opens the report as the form it exports as** (`dashboard.school-head.reports.view` → `schoolhead-dashboard/report-view.blade.php`). Baseline and Endline render the DepEd Nutritional Assessment grid from **`BmiAssessmentReport`** — the one computation the coordinator's SBFP Forms page and the workbook exporter also read, so the form on screen and the file that downloads cannot report different numbers for the same school. Masterlist renders the masterlist form; a monthly report renders its accomplishment table. The page inlines `feeding-sbfp-forms.css` so the facsimile keeps its own register rather than being restyled, and it carries `partials/schoolhead-live`: the figures are derived, so an adviser's weighing recorded while it is open changes what it says.

   **A report is exportable only once its weighing is finished.** `readiness()` is the one rule — baseline needs every learner on the roll measured, endline every beneficiary, the packet both — and it is read by the tab (which offers View but not Export), the view page (which says what is outstanding) and `export()` (which refuses the download). A form with learners still unmeasured is not a draft of the school's return, it is a form that would be wrong when it was handed in; and a button that is not drawn is not a guarantee, so the endpoint checks too. Monthly reports are never gated — they record a month that has already happened. **An export is the school's DepEd form, not a dump of the screen** (`writeAssessmentForm`): the school heading and address line, one ruled BMI grid per covered grade plus the OVERALL grid, then the Prepared by / Attested by / Noted by block — the same sheet the coordinator's SBFP Forms page prints, so a download can be handed in rather than retyped onto the real form. The cells come from **`App\Support\BmiAssessmentReport`**, the one computation both screens read, so the printed form and the exported one cannot report different numbers for the same school and year. The heading is `App\Support\SchoolLetterhead`'s — Republic / Department / Region / Schools Division, the school and its address — the same class the coordinator's printed form and masterlist export read, so all three head the same school the same way. The signature lines carry the school's own staff through `App\Support\SchoolSignatories` (nurse prepares, head notes) and a name the app does not hold prints as a blank line to sign — never an invented signatory. The export carries no review block — there is no longer a decision to print. The learner list and the outcome figures live on their own **Supporting Data** sheet, so the first sheet stays the form. Exports are XLSX via OpenSpout (the packet is one workbook: Baseline BMI, Final BMI, Accomplishment, Supporting Data); PDF is the print block, as everywhere else in this app. `SchoolHeadReportExportTest` reads the workbook back and guards all of it.
3a. **Improved / remained wasted / regressed is one split, read by both roles.** `FeedingNutritionProgress::split()` turns the counts `build()` and `SchoolHeadOverview::outcome()` already produce into five segments — improved, remained wasted (same rung), regressed (dropped a rung), above normal (measured, no rung to compare) and **not yet measured** — every share over **all** beneficiaries, the same denominator the improvement rate uses, so the segments sum to the roll and a split over five endlines cannot read "60% improved". One partial (`partials/outcome-split`) draws it on the coordinator's Nutritional Progress panel and the head's Reports ("Programme Outcome"); never compute the shares a second time in a view. `FeedingOutcomeAnalyticsTest` guards it.
3b. **Predictive analytics is gated on history, and says so** (`App\Support\FeedingProgramForecast` → `partials/program-outlook` on the head's Reports). A cycle counts as completed when it is a *prior* school year with at least one endline on record — the running year is listed, never fitted — and nothing is projected below `MIN_CYCLES` (2): the panel prints how many cycles it has and needs instead, because a trend through one point is a guess with a chart behind it. Above that, the next year's beneficiaries and improvement rate are a least-squares line through the completed cycles, every input is printed in the history table under the figures, the projected row is tinted and labelled *Projection*, and **no peso rate is compiled in** — the projected feeding days are the roll × `FeedingProgramCycle::durationForInstitution()`, and the Division's per-meal rate is the reader's to apply. It reads each year through `SchoolHeadOverview::for()`, so its history is the history the Reports tab prints.
4a. **The outcome cards report the closing weigh-in, and say which children they mean.** Rehabilitated and Rehabilitation Rate were removed. What remains is **Still Severely Wasted and Wasted** — beneficiaries whose *endline* reading is on the wasting scale (`outcome()['still_wasted']`) — beside Improved and Gain at Normal. It is deliberately **not** `still_undernourished`, which is every beneficiary short of Normal and therefore sweeps in the ones nobody re-measured: a child no one has weighed is not evidence either way, so they are counted on the card's hint as "not yet measured" instead. Improvement and rehabilitation stay separate concepts in `SchoolHeadOverview::outcome()` even though the cards no longer print the latter.

5. **Masterlist is read-only and says so, and the School Nurse reads it too** (`SchoolHeadMasterlistController`). `READ_ROLES` is the whole guard — `school_head` and `school_nurse`. The nurse already opens every learner's health record one at a time, and "how many children are wasted this year" had no screen that answered it; the page writes nothing for anybody, so widening the *reader* moves nothing about who may change a measurement (still the class adviser's) or an enrolment (the coordinator's). It is **one controller and one reading rendered in whichever rail the reader belongs to** — the nurse's `.sb-*` rail with `partials/nurse-page-transition`, the head's `.asb-*` rail with `partials/role-page-transition` — never a second copy that could report different figures. The nurse's URL is `dashboard.school-nurse.nutritional-status`, under their own path rather than the head's, because `EnsureActiveSession` seeds a prototype session for whichever role a URL belongs to and a demo nurse on a `/dashboard/school-head` URL would be re-seeded as the head before the page rendered. `NurseLusogShellTest` guards the nurse's half. It is titled **Nutritional Health Status** on the rail, in the tab, in the breadcrumb and on the print masthead — "of All Learners" was dropped from all four, so the tab has one name. Eight filters in the two kinds the coordinator's tabs use — school year, grade and section are scope; sex, baseline, latest, beneficiary standing and attendance standing narrow the list — and every one but the school year is applied in PHP because its column is encrypted. `not_measured` is a real filter answer. **The two weighings are two column groups, each named on the table**: Baseline and Endline both carry Weight / Height / BMI / Status / Height-for-Age, and a phase nobody recorded is an em dash rather than the other phase's figures. Height-for-age is read through `NutritionalHealthStatus::forRecord()` — the student profile's own reading (the adviser's stored classification at baseline, computed from endline height and age at endline) — so the list and a learner's tab cannot classify one child two ways; both downloads carry it. One column used to print the endline if there was one, else the current record, else the baseline — which means a different reading on every row, cannot be compared down, and gives a head no way to tell which weighing they are looking at. The row still carries that fallback figure (`weight` / `height` / `bmi`) because the sort reads it, but nothing renders it, and the export writes both weighings under their own headings. **The endline Status is an endline weighing's own, or "Not measured"** (`measuredEndline()`: no endline height or weight, no endline status — the old Fill Medical Record stamped one on rows nobody re-weighed), and **Change compares baseline with that endline**, so with no endline it reads "Not measured", never "No change". The Latest status filter still reads the latest weighing on file. A learner's record opens from the whole name **cell** into a dialog cloned from that row's own `<template>`, so the panel and the row are one render; it has no edit control anywhere, because measurements belong to the class adviser and enrolment to the Feeding Coordinator. **Print list downloads the masterlist form, not the browser's print dialog** (`printList`, one GET route per reader's path): the Feeding Coordinator's Export Masterlist layout, written through the same `App\Support\MasterlistSheet`, with this table's own columns for the weighing on screen and the active filters named on the S.Y. line. It is a GET because the head is refused every POST, so the browser's search and sort travel as `ids` — the visible rows in screen order, sent only when either is in play — and can only choose among rows `build()` already allowed; an empty `ids` prints the empty form, never the whole school. Export list beside it is the plain data table and is a separate document. `NutritionalStatusPrintListTest` guards it.
6. **Health Overview is the clinic, aggregated** (`SchoolHeadHealthController` → `schoolhead-dashboard/health.blade.php`). Four cards, a monthly activity chart on a clean axis (`axisMax()` steps 1/2/5 × 10ⁿ), disposition and by-grade meters, complaint category and complaint rankings, and the latest visits. **The clinical narrative never appears**: no learner's name, no treatment, no clinic note text — a head reading a management summary has no need for them and the minimum-necessary principle says they should not be handed them (`SchoolHeadOversightTest` asserts a learner's name and their treatment never render). The chart is one neutral series against an axis; colour carries nothing, because a count of visits has no status scale.
7. **Consent Compliance answers a compliance question, never a clinical one** (`SchoolHeadConsentController`). Four cards, completion by section worst-first (the row a head has to chase is the row at the top), and the outstanding learners with their standing. It reports the *standing* of a form and never its contents — the allergies, the write-in exceptions and the parent's signature belong to the adviser and the nurse who act on them. Export is XLSX via OpenSpout, print is the shared `@media print` block.

   **A health-service filter answers the question the other way round.** The catalogue is `HealthConsentForm::SERVICES` — the printed Sulat-Pahibalo's own list, read through `serviceLabels()` and never re-typed — and choosing one (Deworming, say) lists the learners the school **may actually give that service to**: a parent answered, did not refuse, and the letter covered it. That is `consent()['granted_rows']`, and because `services` and `consent_choice` are encrypted at rest the whole filter runs in PHP after fetch. A refusal, an unanswered letter and an unsent draft all authorise nothing and appear on none of it.

   **One signed letter opens on its own page** (`consent-forms.head-show` → `HealthConsentFormController::headShow`, reusing the nurse's read-only view). Reading a monitoring table and opening a named child's parent's signature are different acts, so the second is a deliberate click, refused for an unsigned form, re-scoped to the head's own school, and recorded twice — in `audit_logs` through the `dashboard/*` pattern and on the form's own audit trail. The table beside it still carries standing and service only. `SchoolHeadConsentServicesTest` guards this.
8. **Inventory Overview is stock and standing, and nothing else** (`SchoolHeadInventoryController`). The role has `medicine.view-inventory` but not receipt or dispensing, so the page renders no receive form, no dispense action and no adjustment — and `RestrictSchoolHeadWrites` refuses those endpoints regardless of what a page renders. **A list of who a medicine went to is deliberately absent**: it names the learner and why, which is clinical information about a child, and "are we running out of paracetamol" does not need it. Consumption reaches this page only through `App\Support\MedicineUsage`, which groups on the plain `medicine_id` / `quantity` / `dispensed_at` columns and cannot answer who received any of it.
9. **The status fills are a validated five-slot set** (`--sh-sw` / `--sh-w` / `--sh-n` / `--sh-ow` / `--sh-ob` in `schoolhead.css`). Normal and Overweight reuse the theme's already-validated `--series-healthy` / `--series-risk` unchanged; the other three were stepped against them and the whole set passes all six dataviz checks on the card surface. Re-run the validator before changing any of them.
